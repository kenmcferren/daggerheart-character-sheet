# Classes

## **Envoy**

Evasion: 11; HP: 5; Stress 6; Domains: Grace & Bone

**Envoy Hope Feature**: Spend any number of Hope warn and/or encourage an ally. They increase their evasion or next action roll by 2 plus the hope you spent until the end of their next spotlight or your next spotlight.

**Squad Goals:** When you move to a point in Close range, mark a Stress to bring up to two willing creatures in Very Close range of your starting point with you. When you attack an enemy, mark a Stress to allow an ally in Very Close range of you to make an attack against the same adversary as a reaction.

**Tactical Mind:** You have Envoy Tokens, to a max of 6\. When you would gain Hope, you may gain an Envoy Token instead. When you long rest, gain an amount equal to your spellcasting trait automatically. You can spend an Envoy token instead of Hope to aid an ally.

#### **Subclass: Commander**

Spellcast Trait: **Presence**

**Foundation Features**

* **Get ‘Em:** You can initiate a number of tag teams equal to your Tier per day. You can spend Envoy tokens in place of Hope for this, one for one.  
* **Stay on Target:** Once per session you can increase the value of another player’s Hope or Fear die by 1\.

**Specialization Feature**

* **You’ve Got This:** When another player rolls with Fear, spend an Envoy Token to let them clear 1d4 Stress.

**Mastery Feature**

* **Focus Fire:** Once per Scene, spend a Hope or an Envoy Token to designate an enemy target for the rest of the scene. Allies attacking that target add \+1d6 to their attacks against that target.

#### **Subclass: Spymaster**

Spellcast Trait: **Presence**

**Foundation Features**

* **In Plain Sight:** Each scene, until you attack that scene, you may use your Presence instead of Finesse to hide and need only props or a small crowd to hide in.  
* **Lie Detector:** Spend an Envoy Token to ask the GM about a specific NPC statement this scene. They must tell you which parts are true and which are lies, but not the extent or contents of any lie.

**Specialization Feature**

* **Every Last Drop**: With a quick conversation you may spend an Envoy Token and an ally may mark a Stress to refresh one of their once per rest or once per day features.

**Mastery Feature**

* **Contingencies**: While you have 2 or more Envoy Tokens, you have a new death move: “**Contingencies:** Spend all Envoy Tokens and all Hope. Describe how an adversary in this scene dies instead, exactly as you planned from the start. Then follow the instructions of the Avoid Death move.”

## **Mystic**

Evasion: 10; HP: 5; Stress 6; Domains: Arcana & Spendor

**Mystic Hope Feature:** Spend 3 Hope to reroll a spellcast roll.

**Mystic Bond:** Each rest you can designate up to 10 creatures to be part of your Mystic Bond. You may mark a Stress to let one of them clear a Stress on each of your spotlights.

**Vitality Network:** You have a pool of Vitality Points (VP). After a long rest, refill it to its maximum value of your Spellcasting Trait \+ your Tier. After your action roll you may spend 2 VP to allow someone within your Mystic Bond to clear a Stress or clear an HP.

#### **Spellweaver**

Spellcast Trait: **Instinct**

**Foundation Features:**

* **Arcane Font:** When you cast a spell, gain a Vitality Point.  
* **Eldritch Forge:** Spend 3 VP before you cast a spell to gain one of the following effects on a success: (A) increase a die size listed in the spell by 1, (B) increase the range by 1 zone, or (C) cast it as though standing at the location of another Mystic Bond sharer. (A d20 increases to d20+6.)

**Specialization Features:**

* **Magical Refraction:** On a spellcast roll with Hope, gain a Vitality Point.  
* **Crimson Rite:** Spend 2 VP after a successful spellcast roll to gain one of the following effects: (A) increase your proficiency by 1, a (B) choose a second target in range for the spell.

**Mastery Features:**

* **XXX:** When you crit a spellcast roll, refill your VP.  
* **Weaver:** During your spotlight you may choose up to 3 unique effects to apply to a spell provided you can pay VP for them.

#### **Bondsmage**

Spellcast Trait: **Presence**

**Foundation Features:** 

* **Full Hearts:** You refill your Vitality Pool each rest instead of long rest. When you would gain a Hope, you may gain a VP instead.  
* **Guiding Light:** If your action roll was with hope, the cost to clear an ally’s stress or HP is 1 VP instead of 2\.

**Specialization Features:**

* **Empathic Bond:** When an ally within your mystic bond clears more than 1 HP or clears more than 1 stress, gain a VP.  
* **Constant Gardner:** You can use your vitality network twice per spotlight instead of once.

**Mastery Features:**

* **Shared Trauma:** When an ally would mark HP, you may mark a Stress and spend 2 VP to reduce their damage by one threshold.  
* **Sacrificial Act:** When an ally would make a death move, you may switch places with them as a reaction. You make a death move instead, which cannot be Avoid Death. They receive the benefits of a long rest immediately and take the spotlight next. This can’t be interrupted with Fear expenditure.

## **Operative**

Evasion: 12; HP 6; Stress 6; Domains: Midnight & Codex

**Operative Hope Feature:** Spend 2 Hope when you make an action roll to add an experience and gain advantage on the roll.

**Tech Savvy:** Gain an additional domain card at 1st level. On all domain cards, replace the words “Spell” and “Magic” with “Tech”. Thus Rain of Blades is a level one Tech, which does Tech damage. Replace “Grimore” with “Kit”. If you swap out a Tech or Kit card reduce the stress cost to do so by 1\.

**Called Shot:** If you have not moved during your spotlight you can make a called shot. Make an attack roll with a weapon of range Close or farther. On a hit, mark a Stress to deal d4 extra damage using your proficiency. Your spotlight ends after the shot (preventing movement).

#### **Weapons Specialist**

Spellcast Trait: **Agility**

**Foundation Features:**

* **Precision 1:** When you add an experience to a called shot, increase the extra damage to d6 instead of d4.  
* **Pride in your Work:** If you roll a called shot with Hope, gain an additional Hope.

**Specialization Features:**

* **Precision 2:** your called shot bonus damage is always a d6, and now increases to a d8 if you added an experience to it.  
* **Run and Gun:** You can move before, but not after, your called shot.

**Mastery Features:**

* **Baba-yaga:** Your operative hope feature now costs 1 Hope.

#### **Technologist**

Spellcast Trait: **Knowledge**

**Foundation Feature:**

* **Collector**: When you make a tech roll (or weapon roll that does tech damage) with Hope, put a token on this card, up to 6\. Each rest, declare Gadget or Ordnance and then spend all tokens to roll a d20 for each. Keep a number of results equal to your Knowledge Trait. Only you can use these inventions, and they become junk at the start of your next rest.

**Specialization Features:**

* **Tinker:** When rolling for your Collector feature, you may roll 1d12 \+ any number, 1 through 10, instead of 1d20.   
* **Extra Juice**: Mark a Stress. For the rest of the scene, when you deal tech damage to an adversary, they also mark 1 Stress.

**Mastery Feature:**

* **Recycler:** When you use a gadget, you may mark 2 Stress to prevent it from being consumed. At the start of your rest, prevent one gadget or ordnance from falling apart.  
* **Shareable:** You can freely share your inventions, up to one per ally at a time. Sharing more causes previous inventions to fall apart.

## **Solarian**

Evasion: 10; HP: 7; Stress: 6; Domains: Blade & Arcana

**Solarian Hope Feature:** Spend 3 Hope to summon all 3 solar conjurations.

**Twin Energies:** You have two modes, Graviton and Photon, between which you can switch at the start of your spotlight. When you switch, you may perform a Solar Conjuration of a mote, weapon, or aura. Your conjurations take on your mode with each switch.

* **Solar Mote:** Create a floating mote of energy that orbits you. You may choose to fire it at an enemy when you make a ranged attack roll for an additional \+1d6 damage.  
* **Solar Weapon:** Create a d8, melee, tech damage weapon that uses your spellcasting trait. Then choose one: very close range, d10+2 damage, or the feature “**Reliable:** \+1 to attack rolls.” Add your Tier and Spellcasting trait to your damage.  
* **Solar Aura:** Create a cloud of energy churning around you. When you take damage, you can destroy your aura to reduce the threshold by 1\.

#### **Sidereal**

Spellcasting Trait: **Instinct**

**Foundation Features**

* **Warpage:** Your Mote can (Photon) push or (Graviton) pull the target it strikes up to very close range when you attack with hope.  
* **Slingshot:** You may destroy your Weapon to make a movement check with Instinct instead of Agility. With Graviton, you have advantage. With Photon, deal **P**d6 damage to targets in melee before you move.

**Specialist Features**

* **Lensing:** While your Aura is in place, increase your evasion by \+2.  
* **Alignment:** When you attack an enemy directly between you and an ally, either (Photon) you have advantage on the attack action roll, or (Graviton) the target has disadvantage to attack either of you. 

**Mastery Features**

* **Retrograde:** When an enemy attacks you, but before damage is rolled, you can destroy all your conjurations to shift position into Very Close range, causing the attack to miss.  
* **Well:** When a creature moves towards you, you may mark a Stress to either (Graviton) allow them double movement with no roll required, or (Photon) make a Spellcast Reaction roll to stop them half way.

#### **Coronal**

Spellcasting Trait: **Strength**

**Foundation Features**

* **Expulse:** On mode switch, target enemy in Melee range makes a reaction roll (12 \+ Your Spellcast Trait). On a failure, they (Photon) mark 1 HP or (Graviton) mark 1 Stress.  
* **Stellar Hunger:** On a critical hit with your Weapon or Mote, gain 2 Hope instead of 1 and clear 2 stress instead of 1\.

**Specialization Features**

* **Gleaming Blade:** Increase your Solar Weapon damage by 1 size, and add \+2 (d10+2 and d12+4).  
* **Radiant Aura:** Once per rest, destroy your Aura to make a Spellcast roll against all targets in Close range. Deal **P**d4+6 tech damage to each you would succeed against. Deal 1 damage to all others.  
* **Ejection:** Spend a Hope to fire your mote as a (Graviton) **P**d12 Close range attack or a (Photon) Pd10+2 Far range attack using your Spellcast Trait. It is destroyed after the attack.

**Mastery Features:**

* **Supernova (Graviton):** Spend 2 Hope. Make a Spellcast roll. Targets you succeed against are pulled from Far range into Very Close range of you, mark 1 HP and 2 Stress, and are temporarily Restrained.   
* **Supernova (Photon):** Spend 2 Hope. Make a Spellcast roll. Targets within Very Close range you succeed against are pushed to Far range, mark 2 HP and 1 Stress, and are temporarily Vulnerable.

## **Soldier**

Evasion: 10; HP: 8; Domains: Valor & Dread

**Soldier Hope Feature:** While you have more than 4 Hope you can lose a Hope instead of marking an Armor slot.

**Suppression:** When you make a weapon attack, spend a Hope to make your target temporarily Suppressed. While Suppressed, their action rolls have disadvantage, and if they move from their position their spotlight ends. Their suppression ends when they move or voluntarily mark a Stress.

**Dread Abilities:** All Dread domain card spells are now abilities instead. Dread spells that target a creature do no damage. Instead, when you succeed on a weapon attack you can apply the non-damaging effects from the Dread spell to the target instead of the Suppressed condition.

#### **Tactician**

**Foundation Features:**

* **Overwhelm:** You do not need to spend a Hope to apply Suppressed.  
* **Primary Target:** Once per rest you can declare a primary target. Gain a Hope any time your target marks HP. Mark a Stress if your primary target succeeds a reaction roll to cause them to reroll and take the new result.

**Specialization Features:**

* **Quick Swap:** You do not need to mark Stress when you switch weapons on your spotlight, but are still limited to one switch per spotlight.  
* **Relentless Pursuit:** While your Primary Target is Suppressed:   
  * Movement does not end their suppressed condition, and  
  * Your attacks against them have advantage

**Mastery Feature:**

* **Terrifying Barrage:** When you apply Suppressed, you may spend a Hope to apply it to a second target in Very Close range of the first, without limit aside from the Hope cost.

#### **Armor Storm**

**Foundation Features:**

* **Armor Master:** Reduce your Hope Feature limit of 4 Hope down to 2 Hope. Ignore any feature on armor you are wearing that you wish.  
* **Imposition:** Whenever you would make a Presence trait roll, you may make a Strength roll instead.  
* **Quick Stitch:** When you clear any Armor slots during a rest, clear that many armor slots distributed as you like among your allies as well.

**Specialization Features:**

* **Aegis Protocol:** If you spend Hope instead of Armor, you may then also mark an Armor slot to further reduce your damage.  
* **Heavyweight:** While using a Strength or Presence based weapon add your Armor score to your damage.

**Mastery Features:**

* **That’s Impossible:** When you have 2 or more Hope remaining, spend all hope to reduce incoming damage to 0 and negate any effect an attack against you would have applied.

## **Witchwarper**

Evasion 12; HP 5; Stress 6; Domains: Dread & Midnight

**Witchwarper Hope Feature:** Spend 3 Hope to have a spell affect all targets in your Quantum Field.

**Quantum Field:** You have a quantum field. It can be summoned or dismissed at the start of your spotlight. Summon it at a point within far range by marking 1 Stress. It is destroyed if it exceeds Far Range from you. Everywhere within Very Close range of your quantum field center is considered “Within your Field”.

**Warp Casting: You can only select “Spell” domain cards**. With every spell, replace “within Melee/Very Close/Close/Far/Very Far” with the phrase “within your Field”. Spell effects occur only within your Field..

**Wave Function:** At the start of your spotlight you may move the center of your Quantum Field to any point currently within it. During your spotlight you can forgo your own movement to move your Quantum Field to a new point currently within it, and may mark a Stress to do so again.

#### **Neutronic**

Spellcasting Trait: **Instinct**

**Foundation Features**

* **Ties that Bind:** Gain access to the Splendor domain. Choose one additional domain card from it.  
* **Soothing Pulse:** When any target starts their spotlight within your Field, you may have them clear a Stress.

**Specialization Features**

* **Expansion:** Increase the size of your Quantum Field to encompass all points within Close range, instead of Very Close. You can no longer mark a Stress to move it twice during your Spotlight.  
* **Strong Force:** Targets you choose must stop movement when they enter your Field. Targets you choose that start their spotlight within your Field move at half speed.   
* **Slowed Strikes:** As a reaction you can cause any physical damage that passes through your field to reduce by 1 die, or by half if no dice were used.

**Mastery Features**

* **Quantum Tunneling:** Mark 2 Stress to open a wormhole to a point within your field. Creatures and objects passing through emerge on the other side retaining their facing, and onlookers can see through the field in the same direction they are looking (looking north through the entrance looks north through the exit.) The tunnel closes at the end of your next spotlight.

#### **Electronic**

Spellcasting Trait: **Instinct**

**Foundation Features:** 

* **Destructive Nature:** Gain access to the Arcana domain. Choose one additional domain card from it.  
* **Violent Frequencies:** At the start of your spotlight one target Within your Field marks a HP if they fail a Reaction Roll (14).

**Specialization Features:**

* **Probability Cloud:** Mark a Stress as a reaction after any action roll you see to add or subtract your Spellcasting Trait to the result. This doesn’t create or remove critical effects.  
* **Swift Effect:** When you forgo movement to move your Quantum Field, move it to anywhere within Far range of its current position.

**Mastery Features:**

* **Phenomenal Effect:** You can have up to 6 cards in your loadout.  
* **Abrupt Appearance:** When you summon your quantum field, all targets within it must mark 1 HP

## **Mechanic**

Evasion 10; HP 6; Stress 6; Domains: Valor & Sage

**Mechanic Hope Feature:** Spend 3 Hope to recreate your Sidekick. Alternately, you can prepare during a long rest and forgo gaining Hope to summon your Sidekick.

**Trusty Sidekick:** You have an invention of circuits and parts that accompanies you. It is small enough to be carried as a backpack. It has 4 armor slots, plus 1 per Tier. It has evasion equal to your evasion. It can be equipped with any secondary weapon, giving you the benefits of that side weapon as though you had it equipped. It moves with you to stay in Very Close range of you. When its final Armor slot is marked, your Sidekick is destroyed. 

**Machine Whisperer:** Your Sage domain cards do not apply to natural environments. Instead, your domain cards apply to technological and urban systems. Your tracking might work through computer systems; your familiar might be a small drone; your ability to speak with the natural world is instead used to converse with turrets, ship AIs, and databases. Any magical damage from these domain cards is instead tech or physical damage, your choice.

#### **Mechsuit**

Spellcasting Trait: **Knowledge**

**Foundation Features**

* **Armor Up.** Your sidekick can manifest as a mech suit for you. When summoned, clear as many armor slots as your sidekick would normally have instead of creating them with armor slots. You still gain the benefits of their equipped secondary weapon.  
* **Targeting Systems.** Choose one primary weapon you have in inventory each time you summon your sidekick. That weapon now uses your spellcasting trait when making attacks.

**Specialization Features**

* **Patch Up:** If you clear all armor slots during a rest you can summon your Sidekick as well.  
* **Working as Intended:** You can mark a Stress to mark an additional Armor slot when you take damage. Whenever you mark more than 1 Armor slot at a time, gain 1 Hope.

**Mastery Features**

* **One with the Machine:** Your Sidekick is no longer destroyed unless you allow it. Whenever your armor is fully marked, you can spend all Hope to clear that many Armor slots.  
* **Configurable:** When you summon your Sidekick, and at each long rest, choose one of the following features: flying; submersible; resistant to magic; resistant to tech; resistant to physical; advantage on spellcasting; advantage on attacks. Your sidekick loses the last property it had and gains this one.

#### **Drone Controller**

Spellcasting Trait: **Knowledge**

**Foundation Features**

* **Transformer:** When you summon your Sidekick, choose one of the following features for it: Hovering; \+2 Armor Score; \+2 evasion. Its thresholds by tier are 7/15, 11/24, 13/31, 15/40.. Describe its form when you create it.  
* **Telecast:** When you cast a spell you can use your Sidekick as the origin point for it. When you take the spotlight, you can elect to act solely through your sidekick or on your own. If you start your spotlight in Very Close range of it, you can have it follow your movements.

**Specialization Features**

* **More than Meets the Eye:** When you summon your Sidekick, choose one of the following features for it: Sensor Deadzone Compartment; advantage on movement action rolls; advantage on reaction rolls.  
* **Follow-up:** When your Sidekick succeeds with Hope against a Target you could attack without moving, you can attack that target as a reaction.

**Mastery Features**

* **Robot in Disguise:** Mark a Stress to render your Sidekick invisible or disguised from casual observation. If any target scans it, make a Knowledge reaction roll to prevent being detected.  
* **Master of your Craft:** You can resummon your sidekick for 2 Hope instead of 3 Hope.

## **Technomancer**

Evasion 12; HP 5; Stress 6; Domains: Codex & Sage

**Technomancer Hope Feature:** Mark 1 Armor to gain 2 Hope, or spend 2 Hope to clear 1 Armor.

**Machine Whisperer:** Your Sage domain cards do not apply to natural environments. Instead, your domain cards apply to technological and urban systems. Your tracking might work through computer systems; your familiar might be a small drone; your ability to speak with the natural world is instead used to converse with turrets, ship AIs, and databases. Any magical damage from these domain cards is instead tech or physical damage, your choice.

**Versatile Caster:** Mark a Stress or spend a Hope to alter the damage type and/or flavor of any spell. Damage types include magic, tech, and physical. Flavors affect things like Ice or Fire, scary or pleasant, but not things like damage dice, ranges or durations.

#### **Tinker**

Spellcasting Trait: **Instinct**

**Foundation Features**

* **Always Working:** Each rest, in addition to your rest actions, you may roll a d20 on either the Cypher or Gadget consumables page and gain that object. You can hold up to 4 such objects at any time.  
* **Improvised Magic:** When you cast a spell you can spend a Cypher or Gadget to pay any cost associated with the spell.

**Specialization Features**

* **Efficiency:** When you use **Always Working**, roll 3d20 on one table and 1d20 on the other. Keep any 3 of the 4 results.  
* **Storage Improvement:** You can hold up to 6 such objects instead of 4\.

**Mastery Features**

* **The Right Tool:** Sacrifice a Cypher or Gadget to gain advantage on a Spellcasting Action Roll.

#### **Overwatch**

Spellcast Trait: **Instinct**

**Foundation Feature**

* **Magical Battery:** When you or an ally casts a spell, place a token on this card. You can hold a max of 6\. When an ally in Close range makes an Action or Reaction roll, spend tokens for a \+1 bonus each on the result of their roll. You can do so after the roll but before consequences have been announced.

**Specialization Feature**

* **Capacitance:** If you have no Magical Battery tokens left, the first time you would gain a token gain 2 instead. Increase your maximum token count to 8\.  
* **Shielding:** Spend tokens to add \+1 per token to both thresholds of an ally in Close range. This new threshold lasts the rest of the scene, or until you use this effect again.

**Mastery Feature**

* **Defrag:** When you cast a spell you can swap it with another in your Vault for 1 less Stress than it would normally cost.  
* **Overclock:** When you use your **Versatile Caster** class feature, you may also (A) extend the range by one zone, (B) reduce the cost by 1 Stress or Hope, or (C) increase the damage die used along this track: d4 \-\> d6 \-\> d8 \-\> d12 \-\> d12+d4 \-\> d20 \-\> d20+d6. 

## **Biohacker**

Evasion 11; HP 7; Stress 6; Domains: Bone & Splendor

**Biohacker Hope Feature:** Spend 3 hope to refill your Serum Pool.

**Serum Pool:** You have a pool of Serum Tokens that can hold tokens equal to your Tier plus your Spellcasting trait. Once per spotlight you can deliver a dose of serum with it by succeeding on a weapon attack or a spellcasting action (14) and then spending a Serum Token. Some creatures (at GMs discretion) may have a reaction to negate with difficulty equal to the result of your action roll.

**Tech Caster:** Any Magic damage is instead Tech damage. Your Bone domain cards likely show up on you as biomodifications.

**Minor Serums:** When you deliver a dose of serum pick one of the following temporary effects to apply to a single one of your targets. Lingering effects last the rest of this scene or until the GM spends a fear to end them. Only the most recent serum per target applies.

* The target clears or marks a Stress.  
* The target’s difficulty or evasion is modified by \+2/-2.  
* A PCs trait is improved by \+1.

#### **Gene Splicer**

Spellcasting Trait: **Knowledge**

**Foundation Features**

* **Mutative Effect:** Each long rest you can splice the genes of a willing ally. Give them a copy of any Bone domain card in your vault or loadout. It counts as a transformation for them, and is removed at their next long rest.  
* **Self Help:** Improve any one trait each long rest by \+1. The trait you choose must be different from your most recently improved trait, and applying it removes the previous improvement.

**Specialization Feature**

* **Creature Feature:** Gain a new experience: “Form of \_\_\_\_\_\_\_ \+3”. When you succeed on a weapon attack against an adversary, or when you examine their corpse, spend a serum token to take a sample of it. Replace any name in the blank with the name of the sampled creature, and use it when their form would help you.

**Mastery Feature**

* **I love my work:** Creature Feature does not cost a Serum Token to use. When you make an action roll with Hope, you can gain 2 Serum Tokens instead of 1 Hope.  
* **Cataloger:** You have a log of creatures you’ve sampled with a number of slots equal to your Tier \+ your Knowledge. You can replace any name with any new sample you take. Mark a Stress to update your “Form of \_\_\_\_\_\_” experience to a name on your list.

#### **Physiologist**

Spellcasting Trait: **Knowledge**

**Foundation Features**

* **Intermediate Serums:** Add to your list of serum effects the following:  
  * The target’s reaction rolls have advantage/disadvantage.  
  * The target sees in the dark.  
  * The target changes color.  
  * The target gains a Hope.  
  * The target marks 1 HP.  
* **Chemical Cocktail:** You can spend 2 serum tokens to affect the same target twice. Your minor serum effects can stack.

**Specialization Features**

* **Ample Ampules:** If you start a scene with fewer than 2 serum tokens, refill to 2 serum tokens.  
* **Extra Strength:** Serum effects last until your next long rest.

**Mastery Features**

* **Aerosol Dispersal:** Spend 3 Serum Tokens to impact all creatures in Very Close range with an effect of your choice.  
* **Master Serums:** Add to your list of serum effects the following:  
  * The target falls asleep.  
  * The target has disadvantage on action rolls.  
  * The target is rendered unable to sense other creatures, and other creatures are hidden from it.  
  * The target marks 1 HP.

## **Aspis Sleeper**

Evasion 13; HP 6; Stress 6; Domains: Blade & Grace

**Aspis Sleeper Hope Feature:**

**Force of Will:** When you succeed with Fear on a Grace domain ability against one or more targets, a target of your choice you succeeded against marks a Stress.

**Buried Protocols:** You gain a new experience at \+1 called “Buried Protocols”. It increases by \+1 each time you increase your Tier, and can be increased via leveling options as well, to a max of \+7. Once per rest when you succeed on a roll while using it you can clear a Stress and gain a Hope.

#### **Infiltrator**

Spellcasting Trait: **Finesse**

**Foundation Features**

* **Undercover:** The first time each scene you succeed at a Finesse action roll the GM loses 1 fear if they have more than 3 fear.  
* **Who, me?:** When a single adversary would sense you while you are Hidden you can mark a Stress to make a Spellcasting reaction roll against them. On a success they seem to forget what they saw or heard.

**Specialization Features**

* **Quickdraw:** You can hide any one handed weapon on your person. If the weapon is hidden at the start of your spotlight, your first attack roll with it has advantage.  
* **Escape Route:** Spend a Hope to unlock a mundane lock, loosen a panel, slip by a single guard, or otherwise facilitate a simple step in an infiltration plan.

**Mastery Features**

* **Backstory:** When you succeed on a Presence roll or a non-attack Spellcast roll against an NPC you can spend a Hope to have them give you a perfect backstory. (i.e. “You must be here for the gala.”)  
* **Desperation:** Gain a new experience called “Desperation”. It has a bonus equal to your marked HP.

#### **Executioner**

Spellcasting Trait: **Agility**

**Foundation Features**

* **Brutal Assault:** when you damage a target you can mark a Stress to force them to mark 1 more HP.  
* **Contract:** When you use your Buried Protocols experience for an attack roll, add its bonus to your damage.

**Specialization Feature**

* **Take Down:** Before rolling an attack you can spend a Hope to declare a Take Down. If your attack then does Severe Damage the target is taken out of the fight. The GM can spend 2 Fear to keep them in the fight at 1 HP.

**Mastery Features**

* **Fast Learner:** When an adversary succeeds on an attack against you, you can designate it as your Primary Threat. When your Primary Threat subsequently attacks you, reduce the damage you take by 1 threshold, to a minimum of 1\.  
* **Collateral:** Once per scene if your attack defeats an adversary you can roll your standard damage of that attack against another adversary in range.
