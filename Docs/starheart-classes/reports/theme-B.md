## Sage (all 21 cards read)
Nature magic is the genre the drafts rule out, and 17 of 21 cards say so in their text:

| Group | Cards |
|---|---|
| Plants, vines, roots | Vicious Entangle, Towering Stalk, Healing Field, Thorn Skin, Wild Fortress, Plant Dominion, Fane of the Wilds |
| Animals, spirits, sprites | Nature's Tongue, Natural Familiar, Conjure Swarm (beetles), Forest Sprites, Force of Nature (nature spirit), Wild Surge |
| Mounts and weather | Conjured Steeds (horses, camels, elephants), Tempest, Rejuvenation Barrier, Death Grip |
| Fine as is | Gifted Tracker, Corrosive Projectile, Forager, Sage-Touched |

Mechanic and Technomancer already carry Machine Whisperer, which moves Sage cards to technological and urban systems and converts magic damage to Tech or physical. It covers tracking, familiars and speaking to nature, but not roots and vines, steeds, nature spirits or swarms. That is the only real hole in the part B classes.
