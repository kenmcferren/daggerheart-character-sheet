## How cards were judged
Starfinder has magic (mystics, witchwarpers, technomancers, resurrection, healing, necromancy), psionics and tech, so spells as such are on-theme. A card is flagged when its text pulls a different genre: gods or divine rites, undead and necrotic magic, summoned fiends or creatures, spellbooks, runes and sigils, nature magic, or swords-and-sorcery set pieces. Class reskin text already in the drafts: Operative Tech Savvy (Spell/Magic -> Tech, Grimoire -> Kit), Soldier Dread Abilities (Dread spells become abilities), Solarian Solar Weapon (Tech damage). Other classes have none.

## Decision list
- TA1. Envoy: no flagged cards. (A) no change (recommended).

- TA4. Solarian: add to Twin Energies or Solar Weapon: "Magic damage from your Arcana cards is Tech damage." (about 9 words). (A) add (recommended) (B) no change.
- TA5. Soldier bans: Wall of Hunger, Dark Army, Horror cannot be taken (they summon or build things that Dread Abilities cannot turn into abilities). (A) ban these three (recommended) (B) also ban Damnation and Avatar of Terror (C) no ban.
- TA6. Soldier flavor line added to Dread Abilities: "Describe Dread effects as fear, suppression, and pressure, not necromancy." (about 11 words). (A) add (recommended) (B) no change.
- TA7. Soldier gap: add to Dread Abilities: "Dread spells that do not target a creature work as written but count as abilities." (about 15 words; covers Umbral Veil, Spectral Mist, Avatar of Terror after TA5). (A) add (recommended) (B) leave unstated.
