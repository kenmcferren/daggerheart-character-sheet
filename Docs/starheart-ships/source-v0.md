## **Creating a Ship**

Ships are defined by their Size, Configuration, their Frame, and their Domains.

1. For a starting character, your size is 2, Fighter, with a party ship of size 3, catamaran.  
2. Your **configuration** determines the maximum number of system cards you can have in each system. Choose a configuration.

|                   |  Max System Cards   |             |                 |                   |
| :---------------- | :-----------------: | :---------: | :-------------: | :---------------: |
| **Configuration** |    **Transport**    | **Weapons** |   **Science**   |  **Engineering**  |
|                   | Dodge, haul, escape |    Hurt,    |  Sense, Lock,   |  Debuff, Utility  |
|                   |                     |   Inhibit   |      Buff       |                   |
| Interceptor       |          4          |      4      |        2        |         2         |
| Scout             |          4          |      2      |        4        |         2         |
| Sapper            |          4          |      2      |        2        |         4         |
| Surgeon           |          2          |      4      |        4        |         2         |
| Bandit            |          2          |      4      |        2        |         4         |
| Warden            |          2          |      2      |        4        |         4         |

3. Your **frame** determines your stress, extra HP, base evasion, and base thresholds. Choose a frame.

|  Frame   | Free HP | Stress | Evasion | Thresholds |
| :------: | :-----: | :----: | :-----: | :--------: |
|   Bare   |    2    |   8    |   15    |    7/16    |
| Standard |    3    |   7    |   13    |    9/20    |
|  Plated  |    4    |   6    |   11    |   11/24    |
|  Bulky   |    5    |   5    |    9    |   13/28    |

4. Finally, choose your two **domains**.

|   Domain   | Theme                     | Special rules                   |
| :--------: | ------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Flesh**  | Regen, care, fluid        | Plays with Hope more often      |
|  **Mind**  | Think, Create, Utilize    | Analysis tokens, magical damage |
|  **Void**  | Sap, Freeze, Warp         | Plays with Fear more often      |
| **Energy** | Zap, Electrify, Burn      | Power Tokens                    |
| **Metal**  | Strengthen, Push, Protect | Plays with Hull Points          |
| **Glass**  | Study, Cut, Reflect       | Studied Target                  |

5. Pick your domain cards for each system. You will have a total of 6 domain cards. All will be in your loadout. Domain cards have to come from your chosen domains, and can’t exceed the systems maximums for your configuration.  
6. All ships start with 1 HP in each system. Add another HP for each domain card (10hp total), and if any domain cards grant HP add these as well. Finally distribute your Free HP as you would like (between 12 and 15 HP). No individual system can have more than 7 HP.  
7. Name and describe your ship. How did you come by it?

## **Ship Domain Cards**

Keywords

* **While Available:** If this card is in your ship’s loadout or vault you gain a passive benefit from it.  
* **Lock/Locked:** Any ship can make a Science check to lock onto a target of their size or larger. Only one adversary in play can have the Locked condition, and only one PC in play can have the Locked condition. Cards that use it will have the phrase “With Lock:”, allowing proceeding features to be used with the locked ship as the target.  
* **Forward Facing:** During Chases, Forward Facing weapons can only affect ships in front of your ship’s current band. In Maelstroms, these abilities can be used on any ship.  
* **Aft Facing:** During Chases, Aft Facing weapons can only affect ships behind your ship’s current band. In Maelstroms these abilities can be used on any ship.  
* **Observing:** ships that have left an ongoing chase are Observing. They can’t be targeted by ships in the chase. If they want to enter the chase again on their spotlight they can do so at any range band.  
* **Docking/Docked:** Two ships that are physically touching and docked are docked to each other.   
  * Major and Severe Hull damage to one ship allows that ship to pass one of the Hull damage on to the other ship.   
  * Docked ships cannot make Transport action rolls if the ship to which they are docked is the same size or larger than their own.  
* **Cloud Effects:** A Cloud Effect is an area of space with some kind of effect present within it. Unless otherwise stated, Clouds have these properties:  
  * Clouds have a center point at your position when created.  
  * Clouds have a radius which depends on the Tier of the ship that created it. For Size 1, the radius is melee; for Size 2, Very Close; for Size 3 and 4, Close; and for Size 5, Far.  
  * Clouds are stationary when created in Maelstroms. In Chases they keep their original band until they dissipate.  
  * Clouds dissipate at the start of your next spotlight.  
  * Clouds have no inherent effect until a feature adds one.  
  * You choose which features are active when you create a cloud, but the features don’t change afterwards until you create a new cloud.  
* **Pdx Damage**: Pd6, Pd8, etc are shorthand for “d8 damage using your pilot’s proficiency”.  
* **Sizes:** Ships of different sizes interacting have the following effects  
  * Larger ships cannot normally roll to lock onto smaller ships.  
  * Larger ships have disadvantage when firing on smaller ships.  
  * Smaller ships have advantage when firing on larger ships.  
  * When a ship marks damage, larger ships mark fewer HP, and smaller ships mark more, by 1 point for each size difference they have.

At the start of any of your subsequent spotlights, you can discard the Shrapnel Mine Token to clear a Stress and clear an HP.

**Docking Wench,** *Engineering, Metal Domain.* Your docking attempts have advantage. Attempts to undock from your ship have disadvantage if you so choose.

**Counterfire,** *Engineering, Metal Domain.* This card can hold 2 Reload Tokens. Spend any number Reload Tokens as a reaction to an enemy attack roll, before damage is rolled. Each token spent reduces enemy damage by 1 die, to a minimum of 1 die. **While Available:** You can spend a Hope or mark a Stress to refill any card’s Reload Tokens.

**Undercoat,** *Engineering, Metal Domain.* At the start of your spotlight your thresholds become equal to your base thresholds plus the number of HP you have marked.

**Lightweight Frame,** *Engineering, Metal Domain.* At the start of your spotlight increase your base evasion by \+2 for each system with fully marked HP (Transport, Weapons, Engineering, and Science.)

**Orbiting Plate,** *Engineering, Metal Domain.* **Mark a HP** to create an Orbiting Plate. When a direct attack hits you with less than 5 over your evasion you may destroy the Orbiting Plate to negate its damage. During your spotlight, if it has not been destroyed, you may destroy it to clear a HP instead.

**Projection Field,** *Engineering, Glass Domain.* Your Studied Target may be considered to be one range band closer to you and any Allies you choose when making action rolls that target entities.

**Parallax Protocol,** *Engineering, Glass Domain.* **Mark a Stress** when an enemy attacks you to increase your Evasion by \+3 against this attack. If the attacker is your Studied Target, you may then **clear a Stress**.

**Ultraviolet Imaging,** *Engineering, Glass Domain.* Spend a Hope to make an Engineering roll against a target in Very Far range. On a success, this target is *Highlighted* until they leave Very Far range or until you apply this condition to another target. While *Highlighted* they cannot hide or cloak without full physical obstruction and they are *Vulnerable.* If you made this roll with Hope you may also make them your Studied Target.
