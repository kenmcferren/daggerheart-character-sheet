# Ship archetypes: owner's source list (2026-11-08)

Verbatim from the owner. A ship's two domains pick one archetype (15 pairs from 6 domains). Text is not yet edited for print; see the Series 15 plan in `Docs/plan.md` for the questions.

Flesh + Mind - Leviathan

* While you have any Hope, gain a +1 to all system action rolls.
* While you are out of Hope, gain +1 to all system reaction rolls.

Flesh + Void - Necromancer

* Once per scene, as a reaction, when a ship in Very Close range becomes disabled you can spend a Hope to clear a HP.
* Once per scene you may spend a GM Fear as though it was Hope belonging to you.

Flesh + Energy - Drake

* When you damage an enemy within Very Close range, gain a Power Token.
* Once per spotlight you can spend 2 Power Tokens in place of 1 Hope.

Flesh + Metal - Champion

* Once per scene, when you mark a single Hull Point, you can spend a Hope instead.
* When an ally in Very Close range would take damage from a direct attack you can take that damage instead as a reaction.

Flesh + Glass - Direwolf

* Once per scene, when your Studied Target becomes disabled, gain 3 Hope. Choose a new Studied Target immediately.

Mind + Void - Wraith

* If you have no Analysis Tokens, gain an Analysis Token when the GM spends a Fear on an adversary activation.
* Discard all Analysis Tokens before a ship system roll to gain a bonus equal to the number discarded.

Mind + Energy - Elemental

* Once per spotlight you can convert an Analysis Token to a Power Token or vice versa.
* Spend a Power Token to gain tech resistance against one instance of damage.

Mind + Metal - Golem

* When you mark any number of HP due to an adversary attack, gain 1 Analysis Token.
* When you would take damage, discard any number of Analysis Tokens to reduce the damage by 1 per character tier per Token.

Mind + Glass - Alchemist

* Spend up to 3 Analysis Tokens before you make an Action roll against your Studied Target to gain one of the following benefits for each spent: (A) convert the damage type to any damage type; (B) Increase the proficiency of the attack by 1; or (C) extend the range of your attack by one band, to a maximum of Very Far. Each can be chosen only once.
* When an Action Roll against your Studied Target succeeds, gain an Analysis Token.

Void + Energy - Specter

* Spend 3 Power Tokens to become transdimensional until the start of your next spotlight. While in this state you can move through solid objects, do not take damage, and cannot cause damage. If you end this state in contact with a physical object, mark 3 HP and a Stress and place yourself adjacent to it.

Void + Metal - Revenant

* When you mark any number of Hull Points you may also reload one domain card.
* Your proficiency increases by the number of systems you have that are Breached.

Void + Glass - Lich

* While you are within Close range of your Studied Target, each time the GM spends a fear to spotlight it they must also mark an unmarked Stress on it.
* Once per refuel clear Stress equal to the GM's fear pool.

Energy + Metal - Phoenix

* When you mark any number of HP, gain that many Power Tokens.
* Once per spotlight, when you would mark HP, spend 3 Power Tokens to reduce the number by 1.

Energy + Glass - Raiju

* Spend 2 Power Tokens to move a Far Distance directly toward your Studied Target before making an Action roll. If this roll results in damage to your target it also marks a Stress. With Lock: Up to Very Far distance instead.
* When you move closer to your Studied Target or they move closer to you, gain a Power Token.

Metal + Glass - Chiurgeon

* If your attack against your Studied Target would fail, mark a Hull Point to cause it to succeed with Fear instead.
* Once per refuel when your Studied Target marks Hull Points within Very Close range, clear a number of Hull Points that they marked.
