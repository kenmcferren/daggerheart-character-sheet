# Starheart Ships (working text)

Series 14 working copy. Rules section: Batch 0b; domain descriptions approved (Batch 5). Cards: Batches 1-4, cross-system pass Batches 5 and 5b (all 2026-11-07).

## Ships

A ship has a size, a configuration, two traits, a frame and two domains. The **pilot** is the character flying it. Each starting character has a size 2 Solo Flier, and the party shares a size 3 **Hub Ship**.

| Size | Name |
| :-: | :-- |
| 1 | Drone |
| 2 | Solo Flier (Flier) |
| 3 | Catamaran |
| 4 | Cruiser |
| 5 | Dreadnought |

### Systems
A ship has four systems: **Transport**, **Weapons**, **Science** and **Engineering**. Each ship domain card belongs to one system, and each system has its own Hull Points.
- **Transport:** moving, piloting, escaping and docking.
- **Weapons:** attacks.
- **Science:** sensing, analysis, communications and Locks.
- **Engineering:** other ship equipment.

Rolls for a system are **Transport Action Rolls**, **Weapons Reaction Rolls**, and so on. A "Transport roll" (or Weapons roll, and so on) means either an Action Roll or a Reaction Roll for that system; cards name one only when it matters.

## Creating a Ship
Build a Solo Flier and the Hub Ship with the same steps.

1. **Configuration.** Choose a configuration. It sets the most domain cards each system can hold. The two systems with a maximum of 4 are your **primary systems**; the other two are your **secondary systems**.

| Configuration | Transport | Weapons | Science | Engineering |
| :-- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Interceptor | 4 | 4 | 2 | 2 |
| Scout | 4 | 2 | 4 | 2 |
| Sapper | 4 | 2 | 2 | 4 |
| Surgeon | 2 | 4 | 4 | 2 |
| Bandit | 2 | 4 | 2 | 4 |
| Warden | 2 | 2 | 4 | 4 |

2. **Traits.** Choose one character trait (Agility, Strength, Finesse, Instinct, Presence or Knowledge) as your **Primary Trait** and a different one as your **Secondary Trait**. Rolls for a primary system use the pilot's Primary Trait; rolls for a secondary system use the pilot's Secondary Trait. A different pilot uses their own trait values.

3. **Frame.** Choose a frame. It sets your Free HP, Stress slots, Evasion and damage thresholds.

| Frame | Free HP | Stress | Evasion | Thresholds |
| :-- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Bare | 2 | 8 | 15 | 7/16 |
| Standard | 3 | 7 | 13 | 9/20 |
| Plated | 4 | 6 | 11 | 11/24 |
| Bulky | 5 | 5 | 9 | 13/28 |

4. **Domains.** Choose two domains. The descriptions are shown in the creator, not printed.
   - **Flesh** (Hope): Living ships, grown, bred or tamed rather than built. They heal their own wounds, flex around danger and feel what their crew feels.
   - **Mind** (Analysis Tokens, magic damage): Magical ships, carved with runes, crewed by spellcasters or run by a thinking core. They calculate, conjure and see what others miss.
   - **Void** (Fear): Strange ships, made of things that shouldn't exist or touched by the dark between stars. They bend space, drain warmth and feed on dread.
   - **Energy** (Power Tokens): Charged ships, with reactors running hot, coils humming and plasma vented on a whim. They zap, burn and overload.
   - **Metal** (Hull Points): Armored ships, all heavy plating, rivets and raw mass. They take a beating, ram through and hold the line.
   - **Glass** (Studied Target): Precise ships, built around lenses, prisms and crystal sensors. They study a target, cut it apart and turn its own light against it.

5. **Domain cards.** Choose 6 ship domain cards from your two domains. No system can hold more cards than your configuration allows. All 6 cards are in your loadout. Cards that hold Reload Tokens start full.

6. **Hull Points.** Each system starts with 1 HP. Add 1 HP to a system for each of its cards, plus any HP a card grants. Then place your Free HP in any systems. No system can have more than 7 HP.

7. **Name and describe your ship.** How did you come by it?

## Keywords
- **Pilot:** The character flying the ship. Ships have no Hope: features that spend or gain Hope use the pilot's Hope.
- **Hull Points (HP):** A ship's Hit Points, tracked per system. When a ship marks HP, the pilot chooses which systems mark them, unless an adversary's feature says otherwise.
- **Breached:** A system with all its HP marked is Breached. Every action using a Breached system has disadvantage, whether or not it comes from a domain card.
- **Disabled:** A ship with every HP marked is Disabled. It can't fly, attack or take other ship actions. See Damage.
- **While Available:** If this card is in your ship's loadout or vault, you gain its passive benefit.
- **Sense:** A ship can sense another ship within sensor range (Very Far, unless a domain card changes it) that isn't blocked by other ships or danger zones.
- **Lock / Locked:** See Locks. Features marked "**With Lock:**" need their target to be Locked.
- **Studied Target:** A ship a card has made your Studied Target. You can have one at a time; a new one replaces the old. It ends when the scene ends.
- **Analysis Tokens, Power Tokens:** Domain tokens that cards grant and spend. You can hold up to 12 of each.
- **Reload Tokens:** Some cards hold Reload Tokens, up to the number they list. While a card with Reload Tokens is available, you can spend a Hope or mark a Stress to refill one card's Reload Tokens.
- **Scene end:** Conditions, Clouds, mines and other effects your ship creates end when the scene ends. Tokens last until spent or until your next Refuel or Refit.
- **Contact:** You make contact with a ship when you succeed on an attack against it, dock with it, or succeed on a Transport Action Roll against it.
- **Forward Facing:** In a Chase, Forward Facing features can only affect ships in a band ahead of yours. Outside a Chase they can affect any ship.
- **Aft Facing:** In a Chase, Aft Facing features can only affect ships in a band behind yours. Outside a Chase they can affect any ship.
- **Observing:** A ship that has left a Chase. See Chases.
- **Docked:** See Docking.
- **Cloud:** An area of space with an effect inside it. Unless a feature says otherwise:
  - A Cloud is centered on your position when you create it.
  - Its radius depends on your ship's size: Melee (size 1), Very Close (size 2), Close (sizes 3 and 4), Far (size 5).
  - It stays where you created it. In a Chase it keeps its band.
  - It dissipates at the start of your next spotlight.
  - It has no effect until a feature adds one. Choose which of your features are active when you create it; they don't change until you create a new Cloud.
- **Pd damage:** "Pd8+4" means roll d8s equal to the pilot's Proficiency, then add 4.
- **Size:** When ships of different sizes interact:
  - The larger ship has disadvantage on attacks against the smaller ship; the smaller ship has advantage on attacks against the larger ship.
  - Size also changes the HP a ship marks (see Damage) and limits Locks (see Locks).

## Characters and Ships
Ships are not creatures. Character domain cards and features usually have no effect in ship scenes; the GM decides exceptions (such as Teleport). Once ships are Docked and creatures Board, character abilities work normally inside and between the ships.

## Ranges and Moving
- Ship scenes use the usual ranges (Melee, Very Close, Close, Far, Very Far), scaled to space.
- When you make an action roll, your ship can move to a point within Close range as part of it. To move farther, or to move without making an action roll, succeed on a Transport Action Roll.

## Resting
- **Refuel** (1 day): End any conditions and tokens your ship still has. You can then mark any of your unmarked Stress to pay for domain card features (such as refilling Reload Tokens). Then clear 1d6 Stress and 1d6 HP, spread across your systems as you choose.
- **Refit** (1 week, at a place that can do the work): As a Refuel, but you can mark as much Stress as you want to pay for domain card features. Then clear all Stress and HP. If the situation allows it, the GM can let you swap domain cards.

## Locks
- Make a Science Action Roll against an adversary ship's Difficulty to give it the **Locked** condition. You can only Lock ships your size or larger.
- Once a ship is Locked, any ally can use "With Lock" features against it, whatever their size.
- The party can have one Locked adversary at a time. Locking a new target ends the old Lock. A Lock also ends when no PC ship can sense the target.
- Adversaries can Lock one PC ship at a time.
- **Breaking a Lock:** When an adversary acts to break your party's Lock, the PC ship closest to it makes a Science Reaction Roll against its Difficulty; on a failure, the Lock ends. When a PC ship acts to break a Lock on itself, it makes a Transport Action Roll against the highest Difficulty among adversaries that can sense it. On a success the Lock ends; on a critical success, you can also Lock an adversary you can sense.

## Attacking
An attack is any action meant to deal damage. Weapons cards use Weapons Action Rolls unless they say otherwise.

## Damage
- Roll damage and compare it to the ship's thresholds to find the HP it marks, as for characters.
- **Size difference:** A ship marks 1 fewer HP for each size it is larger than the attacker, and 1 more for each size it is smaller. This can reduce the HP marked to 0. Damage from a ship's Clouds, mines and other features counts as coming from that ship; hazards have no size.
- **PC ships:** The pilot chooses which systems mark the HP. When every HP is marked the ship is Disabled: the crew is alive but the ship can't be operated. A Disabled ship marks Stress instead of HP. When it marks its last Stress, the pilot and the ship each make a death move, and they must choose different ones.
- **Adversary ships:** Each marked HP gives -1 to the ship's action and reaction rolls, to a maximum of -6. Marking every HP leaves the ship Disabled. Marking every Stress destroys it and kills those inside; cargo is ruined unless specially protected.

## Docking
- **Docking** joins two ships so that creatures and cargo can cross. Docking with a willing ship needs no roll, unless the GM calls for a Transport Action Roll under pressure.
- **Forced Docking** against an unwilling ship: make a Transport Action Roll with disadvantage against its Difficulty plus its Transport bonus (or a fitting pilot Experience; see Adversary Ships). Against a Disabled ship, roll against its Difficulty with no bonuses; the GM must spend a Fear to add a pilot Experience.
- When an adversary attempts a Forced Docking on your ship, make a Transport Reaction Roll with advantage. On a success the ships don't dock, and the adversary marks 1 HP. On a failure both ships are Docked.
- All touching ships with the Docked condition are docked to each other.
- A Docked ship can't make Transport Action Rolls while docked to a ship of its size or larger.
- When a Docked ship would mark 2 or more HP, it can pass 1 of them to a ship it is docked to.

## Boarding
- Without a domain card that says otherwise, creatures can only board a Docked ship. Boarding a willing ship needs no roll.
- Boarding an unwilling ship is a scene the GM builds (defenders, locked doors, sealed or vented rooms).

## Undocking
- Willing ships undock with no roll, unless the GM calls for one.
- A Disabled ship can't undock and can't stop another ship from undocking.
- **Breaking free of a Forced Docking:** Make a Transport Action Roll with disadvantage against the other ship's Difficulty. With Hope, the ships undock. With Fear, choose: undock and mark 1 HP, or stay docked. On a failure you stay docked.
- **An adversary breaking free:** Make a Transport Reaction Roll with advantage. On a success it stays docked. On a failure it undocks, but if your Hope Die is higher than your Fear Die it must mark 1 HP to do so.

## Adversary Ships
- Adversary ships use the normal adversary stats, plus a **Transport bonus** and a **Weapons bonus**. The GM can add one of these to the ship's Difficulty without spending a Fear.
- Use the ship's Difficulty for ship rolls, not the pilot's. If the pilot has an Experience higher than the relevant bonus, the GM can add it instead, without spending a Fear.
- *Example:* a ship with Difficulty 10 and a Transport bonus of +2 is hit on a 12. If its pilot has *Slippery +3*, the GM can use that instead: the ship is hit on a 13.

## Chases
A Chase is a special ship scene: every ship is moving the same way toward the same goal. Once any ship tries to leave a scene, any other ship that can fly can start a Chase.

### Starting a Chase
- The **lead ship** is usually the one leaving. Place it near the middle of the table, with room for 3 bands behind it and 2 or 3 ahead.
- **Adversary lead:** Up to two other adversary ships can share its band. Place the rest in the next 3 bands behind it, in whatever order suits the scene; no rolls. Each PC makes a Transport Reaction Roll. Beat every adversary's Difficulty: start in the band just behind the lead. Beat some: start one band further back. Beat none: start in the third band back. Then any PC can move back as many bands as they like.
- **PC lead:** The lead PC makes a Transport Action Roll. Adversaries whose Difficulty is higher than the result can start in any band behind the lead; the rest start 2 bands back. With Hope, the other PCs place their ships in any band behind the lead, after the adversaries. With Fear, the other PCs roll and place as for an adversary lead, before the adversaries.

### During a Chase
- The spotlight moves as in any other scene.
- **Bands** are rows of ships side by side. Within a band, measure range normally. A ship in another band is at least Close away if it is 1 band away, at least Far if 2 bands, and at least Very Far if 3 or more. It can be further, depending on how far it is to the side.
- **Moving:** Changing one band uses your move. To change two bands, make a Transport Action Roll, with disadvantage if any adversary in the Chase is smaller than you. Beat every adversary's Difficulty: move two bands. Beat some: move one band, and one adversary you didn't beat can move one band as a reaction. Beat none: make your normal move within Close range, but you can't move forward a band if an adversary within Close range blocks your advance.
- **Adversaries moving:** When an adversary tries to move more than one band, the closest PC makes a Transport Reaction Roll. On a success, the adversary moves one band and that PC can move one band. On a failure, the adversary moves two bands.
- **Stationary objects** (debris, asteroids) pass through the bands as a column, as wide as the GM decides. Every ship in the column makes the same reaction roll to avoid it.

### Leaving a Chase
- **Dropping out:** Say so on your spotlight. If no adversary pursues, you are Observing. If one does, make a Transport Action Roll against the highest Difficulty among your pursuers; on a success, you are Observing. On a failure, you move one band forward or back, and your pursuer makes the same move.
- **Keeping an adversary in:** When an adversary tries to drop out, one PC can make a Transport Reaction Roll against it. On a failure it is Observing. On a success it moves one band forward or back, and that PC makes the same move.
- **Outpacing:** A ship at Very Far range from every other ship in the Chase is Observing.
- **Observing** ships can't be targeted by ships in the Chase and are usually safe from the Chase's hazards. At the start of its spotlight, an Observing ship can rejoin at either side of any band.

> **Design notes (not printed).**
> - Systems: Transport (dodge, haul, escape), Weapons (hurt, inhibit), Science (sense, lock, buff, heal), Engineering (debuff, utility). Domain themes: Flesh (regen, care, fluid), Mind (think, create, utilize), Void (sap, freeze, warp), Energy (zap, electrify, burn), Metal (strengthen, push, protect), Glass (study, cut, reflect). Before card HP grants a ship has 12-15 HP.
> - Ranges in space, roughly: Melee 0-5 km, Very Close 5-15 km, Close 15-30 km, Far 30-100 km, Very Far 100-300 km. Outside boarding, ships are rarely within a character's Very Far range of each other.
> - Size examples: Drone (0-1 crew; Sputnik, F-15, Farscape-1), Flier (1-2 crew; TNG shuttlecraft, Defiant), Catamaran (2-6 crew, up to 20 passengers; Serenity, Moya, Millennium Falcon), Cruiser (5-100 crew, up to 300 passengers; TNG Enterprise, Klingon Bird of Prey), Dreadnought (100+ crew, up to 10,000 passengers; Star Destroyer, Borg Cube).
> - Open ideas: a cost for Refit work; ways character domain cards could matter in ship scenes; thresholds that change with ship leveling or upgrades; Transport and Weapons bonuses to revisit after playtesting.

**Warp Draw,** *Transport, Void Domain.* **Mark a Stress** to create a Cloud. Ships you choose that start their spotlight in your Cloud mark a Hull Point if they move.

**Power Reroute,** *Transport, Energy Domain.* When you make a non-Transport Action Roll with Hope, you can gain a Power Token. Spend a Power Token to gain advantage on a Transport roll.

#### Metal
**Fleet Transponder,** *Transport, Metal Domain.* Once per Refuel, when an ally within Close range fails a Transport roll with Hope, you can **mark a Stress** to make it a success with Fear instead.

**Cold Steel,** *Transport, Metal Domain.* This card holds 2 Reload Tokens. When you succeed on a Forced Docking, or a ship fails a Forced Docking against you, that ship marks a Stress, and you can spend a Reload Token to make it mark a Hull Point.

**Faraday Cage,** *Transport, Metal Domain.* While any of your Transport Hull Points are unmarked, you have resistance to tech damage.

**Dense Frame,** *Transport, Metal Domain.* Rolls to move your ship against your will have disadvantage, and you have advantage on rolls to resist them. While you are docked only to ships your size, you can make Transport Action Rolls with disadvantage. **While Available:** You have an extra Transport Hull Point.

#### Glass
**Sharp Edges,** *Transport, Glass Domain.* When you break free of a Forced Docking, the ship you were docked to marks a Hull Point. Once per scene, when you make contact with a ship within Melee range, you can **mark a Stress** to make it mark a Hull Point.

**Churn Rider,** *Transport, Glass Domain.* When a larger ship within Very Close range moves, you can **spend a Hope** to move with it as a reaction, to a point within Close range of where you started.

**Mirage Cloud,** *Transport, Glass Domain.* **Mark a Stress** to create a Cloud. Ships in your Cloud can't be targeted by ships at Far range or farther from them. At the start of your spotlight, you can **spend a Hope** to move your active Cloud to your position and keep it from dissipating.

**Reflective Hull,** *Transport, Glass Domain.* When you mark a Transport Hull Point while none are marked, you can make a ship within Very Close range mark a Hull Point. **While Available:** You have an extra Transport Hull Point.

### Weapons

#### Flesh

At the start of each of your spotlights, the Engulfed ship takes Pd6 physical damage.

**Blacklight Laser,** *Weapons, Void Domain.* Make a Weapons Action Roll against a target within Far range. On a success, deal Pd6+4 tech damage. On a roll with Fear, hit or miss, you can **mark a Stress** to Lock the target. **With Lock:** On a success, add the GM's current Fear to the damage.

**Warp Cascade,** *Weapons, Void Domain.* **Mark a Stress** to move to a point within Far range. Then you can make a Weapons Action Roll against a target within Very Close range. On a success, deal Pd10+4 tech damage, and the target is *Restrained* until the end of its next spotlight.

**Warded Hull,** *Science, Mind Domain.* When you would mark Hull Points from damage, you can first spend 2 Analysis Tokens to reduce the severity by one threshold. **While Available:** You have an extra Stress slot.

#### Void
**Charge Extraction,** *Science, Void Domain.* Once during your spotlight, when you make an adversary mark a Stress, you can **spend a Hope** to clear a Stress.

**Chaos Drive,** *Science, Void Domain.* **Mark a Stress** to add the GM's current Fear to a non-Weapons Action Roll.

**Matter Inversion,** *Science, Void Domain.* When you make an action roll with Fear, put an Inversion Token on this card (up to 3). Spend an Inversion Token to choose the damage type of any damage dealt by or to a ship within Very Close range of you.

#### Energy

**Electron Beam,** *Science, Energy Domain.* Make a Science Action Roll (13). On a success, you can spend a Power Token to let a ship within Close range clear 2 Stress. Once per spotlight, you can **mark a Stress** to gain a Power Token.

**Oscillating Power Supply,** *Science, Energy Domain.* Once per spotlight, you can **mark a Stress** to gain 2 Power Tokens, or spend 2 Power Tokens to clear a Stress. **While Available:** You have an extra Stress slot.

#### Metal

**Hull Cannibalization,** *Science, Metal Domain.* Once per Refuel, **mark a Hull Point** to clear 3 Stress. **While Available:** You have an extra Science Hull Point.

#### Glass

**Quantum Entanglement,** *Science, Glass Domain.* Your Studied Target doesn't end until you choose, even when the scene ends. You always know the direction of your Studied Target and how many unmarked Stress it has, at any distance.

**Scanning Array,** *Science, Glass Domain.* **Mark a Stress** to create a Cloud. At the start of their spotlights, allies can Lock any target in your Cloud as a reaction. When the Cloud dissipates, you can make any ship in it your Studied Target.

### Engineering
#### Flesh

**Neural Interface,** *Engineering, Flesh Domain.* When you have no Hope and gain any Hope, clear a Stress. **While Available:** You have an extra Stress slot.

**Corrosive Slime,** *Engineering, Flesh Domain.* Once per spotlight, when you make contact with a ship, you can **mark a Stress** to lower its Major threshold by 2 and its Severe threshold by 4 for the rest of the scene.

**Selective Shedding,** *Engineering, Flesh Domain.* When you make contact with a ship, you can **spend a Hope** to slow it: until the end of its next spotlight, it can only move to a point within Very Close range. In a Chase, it moves back one band instead.

#### Mind

**Arcanic Connection,** *Engineering, Mind Domain.* Once per Refuel, the pilot can cast one of their spells through your ship. Make an Engineering Action Roll in place of the Spellcast Roll. The spell can affect ships your size or smaller as it would affect adversaries, at ship ranges.

**Eldritch Breeze,** *Engineering, Mind Domain.* Make an Engineering Action Roll against a target within Far range. On a success, it is temporarily caught in *Unfair Winds*. When it moves, you can **spend a Hope** or an Analysis Token to push it to a point within Very Close range of where it stopped. If you don't, Unfair Winds ends.

**Rift Generator,** *Engineering, Mind Domain.* **Mark a Stress** to make an Engineering Action Roll (13). On a success, teleport to a point within Very Far range. For each ally within Very Close range you bring with you, mark another Stress or 2 Hull Points.

**Pocket Dimension,** *Engineering, Mind Domain.* **Mark a Stress** to make an Engineering Action Roll (16). On a success, you open a pocket dimension holding an abandoned space station. It lasts long enough for one Refuel, and a Refuel there clears half as much (round down). Characters can rest there undisturbed. Messages can pass in or out only if you allow it.

**Boarding Glyph,** *Engineering, Mind Domain.* **Mark a Stress** to make an Engineering Action Roll against a ship within Very Close range (12 if it is willing). On a success, mark it with a Boarding Glyph and choose a place on your ship. Creatures you allow can travel between that place and a matching place on the marked ship (bridge to bridge, cargo bay to cargo bay). When a creature you forbid tries, make an Engineering Reaction Roll against it; on a success, it takes Pd8 magic damage and doesn't cross. The Glyph lasts while your ship has a pilot, your Engineering system isn't Breached, and the marked ship is within Far range.

#### Void

**Cloaking Field,** *Engineering, Void Domain.* **Mark a Stress** to become *Cloaked* until you make a Weapons or Transport Action Roll. While Cloaked, adversaries can't target you, and your first attack has advantage. Once per adversary spotlight, the GM can spend a Fear to reveal you; you can **spend a Hope** to stay Cloaked.

**Graviton Mine,** *Engineering, Void Domain.* Make an Engineering Action Roll (15). On a success, you can **mark a Stress** and **mark a Hull Point** to place a Graviton Mine at a point within Close range. Other ships within Close range of the Mine are temporarily *Restrained*. Each of them that starts its spotlight within Close range of the Mine makes a Transport Reaction Roll (10 + the GM's current Fear): on a failure, it is pulled to the Mine; on a success, it is no longer Restrained. At the start of your next spotlight, every ship at the Mine takes Pd10+4 physical damage, ignoring size, and the Mine and its Restrained condition end.

**Void Attuned,** *Engineering, Void Domain.* While you have 4 or more Void cards in your loadout, you have resistance to magic damage, and you clear a Stress whenever you take magic damage.

**Scrap Hound,** *Engineering, Void Domain.* Once per Refuel, when an adversary ship is destroyed, you can clear Hull Points equal to 1d4 + its size.

**Veil of Night,** *Engineering, Void Domain.* Make an Engineering Action Roll (13) to create a *Veil of Night*: a flat plane through the battlefield, reaching Very Far range in every direction. When a ship passes through it, you can **mark a Stress** or spend an Analysis Token to make that ship mark a Hull Point. Communications, light, sensing, tech damage and magic damage can't pass through the Veil. You can dismiss it at the end of any spotlight; it ends with the scene.

**Energy Drain,** *Engineering, Energy Domain.* Make an Engineering Action Roll against a ship within Very Close range. On a success, it marks a Stress and you clear a Stress.

**Overload Barrage,** *Engineering, Energy Domain.* **Mark a Stress** to make an Engineering Action Roll against a ship within Close range. #### Metal
