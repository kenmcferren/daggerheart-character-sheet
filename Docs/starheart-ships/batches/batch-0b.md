## Questions
1. Tokens at scene end: your Q8 answer says tokens and conditions a ship creates end when the scene ends. But three cards expect tokens to carry over between scenes: Predictive Pathways ("Each Refuel or Refit, gain 3 Analysis Tokens"), Detailed Systems Readout and Siphon Array ("If you start any scene with no ... Tokens, gain"). Refuel also resets tokens. Should (A) conditions, Clouds, mines and other ship-made effects end at scene end, while tokens last until spent or the next Refuel, or (B) tokens also end at scene end, and those three cards get rewritten in their batches?
2. Refit says "all stress and Hull Points are changed". (A) Read it as "cleared", or (B) did you mean something else?
3. Size and damage: (A) damage from a ship's Clouds, mines and other features counts as coming from that ship, and hazards ignore size, or (B) other?
4. Chase, moving two bands: the rules cover beating all or some adversaries, but not beating none. (A) You still move one band (your normal move), or (B) you don't move?

## Owner answers (2026-11-07), applied to `v1-working.md`
1. A, tokens last until spent or the next Refuel or Refit.
2. A, Refit clears all Stress and HP.
3. A.
4. On a failure you make your normal move within Close range, but can't move forward a band if an adversary within Close range blocks your advance.
