## Where the drafts disagree
- **Stress:** the rules say 6 plus card bonuses; the frame table says 5 to 8.
- **Thresholds:** the rules say "depending on their model (change later?)"; the frame table sets them.
- **HP per system:** the rules say 1, plus 1 if the system has any cards; creation says 1, plus 1 per card, plus Free HP.
- **Science trait:** the rules say all ships use Knowledge for Science; your Q1 answer gives every system the Primary or Secondary Trait.
- **Ship weapons and armor:** the rules mention ship weapons outside domain cards (each with its own trait) and armor cleared on a Refuel or Refit. Creation has neither.
- **Size and damage:** the rules only reduce damage to the larger ship ("Tier 4 - Tier 2"); the Size keyword also adds HP to the smaller ship.
- **Docked damage:** the rules say "would mark more than 1 HP"; the Docking keyword says "Major or Severe damage". These are the same thing (Major = 2 HP, Severe = 3 HP). I'll use the rules wording.
- **Lock:** the keyword says you can only lock ships your size or larger, and one adversary plus one PC ship can be Locked. The rules don't mention size; a Lock ends when the party locks a new target or no PC ship can sense the target.
- **Losing a Lock:** the rules have their own roll for it. The card Sensor Mirage repeats it with a different roll. That goes in Batch 3 (Science).
