# Ship card markup (for review)

Made by `node scripts/srd-convert/starfinder/mark-ship-cards.mjs`. **Bold** = keyword or cost; *italic* = a condition or object the card defines.

## Transport

- **Prey Drive** (flesh): __Mark a Stress__ to add the number of your marked Transport Hull Points to your next Transport Action Roll this spotlight. __While Available:__ You have an extra Stress slot.
- **Writhe** (flesh): While you have any Hope, you have advantage on rolls to make, resist or break free of a __Forced Docking__.
- **Self Repair** (flesh): Once per __Refuel__, __spend up to 3 Hope__ to clear that many Hull Points from one system.
- **Fluid Motion** (flesh): When you make a Transport Action Roll with Hope, you gain +2 Evasion until the start of your next spotlight.
- **Pod Travel** (flesh): When you move, you can make a Transport Action Roll against the highest Difficulty among adversaries within Close range. On a success, you, every ally with Pod Travel available, and one other ally can each move to a point within Close range. On a failure, you move alone.
- **Spore Cloud** (flesh): __Mark a Stress__ to create a __Cloud__. Attacks against targets in your __Cloud__ have disadvantage. __Locked__ ships in your __Cloud__ lose the __Locked__ condition.
- **Predictive Pathways** (mind): When you __Refuel__ or __Refit__, gain 3 __Analysis Tokens__. When another ship moves into Close range of you, you can __spend an Analysis Token__ to move up to Close range away from it as a reaction.
- **Blindspot** (mind): __Mark a Stress__ to slip into the blindspot of a ship within Very Close range. That ship can't target you while you're in its blindspot. This ends when you choose, when either ship starts a spotlight farther than Very Close from the other, or when you use this card on another ship.
- **Engine Sync** (mind): __With Lock:__ Add the trait you use for Transport rolls to your Evasion against the __Locked__ target's attacks, if it's positive.
- **Calculations Confirmed** (mind): Once per scene, when an attack misses you, gain a Hope.
- **Slipstream Transference** (void): When you mark any Hull Points, you can __spend a Hope__ to clear a Stress as a reaction. __While Available:__ You have an extra Transport Hull Point.
- **Warpfield Strain** (void): __Mark a Stress__. Until the end of this spotlight, each adversary with an unmarked Stress that you fly within Very Close range of marks a Stress.
- **Void Cohesion** (void): While you have 4 or more Void cards in your loadout, you have +2 Evasion.
- **Warp Draw** (void): __Mark a Stress__ to create a __Cloud__. Ships you choose that start their spotlight in your __Cloud__ mark a Hull Point if they move.
- **Overdrive** (energy): When you succeed on a roll with Hope, you can __mark a Stress__ or __spend a Power Token__ to move to a point within Close range, even if you've already moved this spotlight.
- **Desperate Charge** (energy): Once per spotlight, __mark a Stress__ to clear a Transport Hull Point. For the rest of this spotlight, add the number of your marked Stress to your Transport rolls.
- **Power Reroute** (energy): When you make a non-Transport Action Roll with Hope, you can gain a __Power Token__. __Spend a Power Token__ to gain advantage on a Transport roll.
- **Fleet Transponder** (metal): Once per __Refuel__, when an ally within Close range fails a Transport roll with Hope, you can __mark a Stress__ to make it a success with Fear instead.
- **Cold Steel** (metal): This card holds 2 __Reload Tokens__. When you succeed on a __Forced Docking__, or a ship fails a __Forced Docking__ against you, that ship marks a Stress, and you can __spend a Reload Token__ to make it mark a Hull Point.
- **Punch Through** (metal): You have advantage on Transport Reaction Rolls. When an environment makes you mark Hull Points, mark 1 fewer. __While Available:__ You have an extra Transport Hull Point.
- **Faraday Cage** (metal): While any of your Transport Hull Points are unmarked, you have resistance to tech damage.
- **Dense Frame** (metal): Rolls to move your ship against your will have disadvantage, and you have advantage on rolls to resist them. While you are docked only to ships your size, you can make Transport Action Rolls with disadvantage. __While Available:__ You have an extra Transport Hull Point.
- **Sharp Edges** (glass): When you break free of a __Forced Docking__, the ship you were docked to marks a Hull Point. Once per scene, when you __make contact__ with a ship within Melee range, you can __mark a Stress__ to make it mark a Hull Point.
- **Churn Rider** (glass): When a larger ship within Very Close range moves, you can __spend a Hope__ to move with it as a reaction, to a point within Close range of where you started.
- **Mirage Cloud** (glass): __Mark a Stress__ to create a __Cloud__. Ships in your __Cloud__ can't be targeted by ships at Far range or farther from them. At the start of your spotlight, you can __spend a Hope__ to move your active __Cloud__ to your position and keep it from dissipating.
- **Defensive Sync** (glass): Once per scene, you can make a ship within Far range your __Studied Target__. You have +1 Evasion against your __Studied Target__'s attacks. When its attack misses you by 1, gain a Hope.
- **Reflective Hull** (glass): When you mark a Transport Hull Point while none are marked, you can make a ship within Very Close range mark a Hull Point. __While Available:__ You have an extra Transport Hull Point.
## Weapons

- **Spearhead Charge** (flesh): __Forward Facing.__ Make a Weapons Action Roll against an unobstructed target within Far range. On a success, move into Melee range of it and deal Pd8 physical damage. __With Lock:__ Roll with advantage.
- **Flechette Spines** (flesh): __Spend a Hope__ to make a Weapons Action Roll against all targets within Close range. Targets you succeed against take 1 physical damage and mark a Stress. If this __Disables__ any ships, gain a Hope.
- **Leeching Strike** (flesh): Make a Weapons Action Roll against a target within Melee range. On a success, deal Pd4+3 physical damage. If it marks any Hull Points, clear a Stress.
- **Engulf** (flesh): __Spend a Hope__ to make a Weapons Action Roll against a target your size or smaller within Close range. On a success, it is temporarily *Engulfed*: it can't make Transport Action Rolls and must target you with all other actions. At the start of each of your spotlights, the *Engulfed* ship takes Pd6 physical damage.
- **Tracer Fire** (mind): __Forward Facing.__ Make a Weapons Action Roll against a target within Far range. On a success, deal Pd6+4 tech damage, and either __spend an Analysis Token__ to __Lock__ the target or gain an __Analysis Token__.
- **Breaching Curse** (mind): __With Lock:__ Make a Weapons Action Roll against the __Locked__ target within Close range. On a success, deal 2d6 magic damage. __Spend any number of Analysis Tokens__ to add a d6 for each one spent.
- **Eldritch Lance** (mind): __Forward Facing.__ Make a Weapons Action Roll against a target within Far range. On a success, choose one: deal Pd6 magic or physical damage, or __mark a Stress__ to deal Pd10+6 magic damage. __With Lock:__ +4 damage.
- **Spacetime Chasm** (void): Make a Weapons Action Roll against a target within Far range. Once per __Refuel__, on a success, __the GM gains a Fear__ and you draw a line from the target to a point within Far range of you and the target. Every ship touching the line takes Pd8+2 magic damage and marks a Stress.
- **Entropic Beam** (void): __Forward Facing.__ Make a Weapons Action Roll against a target within Close range. On a success, deal Pd10+4 tech damage. You can also __mark a Stress__ to make the target mark a Stress and lower its Difficulty by 1 until the end of the scene.
- **Null Sphere** (void): __Mark a Stress__ and choose a point within Far range. Make a Weapons Action Roll (15). On a success, every ship within Very Close range of that point takes Pd6+4 magic damage. Each ship that marks 2 or more Hull Points is also temporarily __Restrained__.
- **Blacklight Laser** (void): Make a Weapons Action Roll against a target within Far range. On a success, deal Pd6+4 tech damage. On a roll with Fear, hit or miss, you can __mark a Stress__ to __Lock__ the target. __With Lock:__ On a success, add the GM's current Fear to the damage.
- **Annihilation Beam** (void): __Forward Facing.__ Once per __Refuel__, __spend a Hope__ to make a Weapons Action Roll against a target within Very Far range. On a success, deal magic damage: a d8 for each Fear the GM has. __With Lock:__ If the target is larger than you, it marks Hull Points as if it were your size.
- **Warp Cascade** (void): __Mark a Stress__ to move to a point within Far range. Then you can make a Weapons Action Roll against a target within Very Close range. On a success, deal Pd10+4 tech damage, and the target is __Restrained__ until the end of its next spotlight.
- **Plasma Caster** (energy): Make a Weapons Action Roll with disadvantage against a target within Far range and against every adversary touching the straight line of your shot. Every ship touching the line marks a Stress. You can __spend a Power Token__ to deal 1d6+10 tech damage to each adversary you succeed against. On a success against the target, it also takes Pd6 tech damage as part of the same attack. __With Lock:__ Roll without disadvantage.
- **Volley Fire** (energy): __Forward Facing. Mark a Stress__ or __spend a Power Token__ to make a Weapons Action Roll against all targets within Close range. Targets you succeed against take 2d6 tech damage.
- **Erratic Pulse** (energy): Make a Weapons Action Roll against a target within Close range. On a success, deal Pd8+4 tech damage, and you can __spend a Power Token__ to make the target temporarily __Vulnerable__. __With Lock:__ You don't need to spend the __Power Token__.
- **Jamming Blast** (energy): Make a Weapons Action Roll against a target within Far range. On a success, the target is temporarily *Jammed*: it has disadvantage on attack rolls. You can also __spend a Power Token__ to deal Pd6 tech damage to it. __With Lock:__ Roll with advantage.
- **Fire Gout** (energy): __Mark a Hull Point__ and gain a __Power Token__ to make a Weapons Action Roll against all targets within Melee range. Targets you succeed against take Pd6+4 tech damage and are *Burning* until they use an action to put it out. A *Burning* ship marks a Stress at the end of each of its spotlights.
- **Tether** (energy): Make a Weapons Action Roll against a target within Melee range. On a success, deal Pd4 tech damage. If it marks any Hull Points, it also marks a Stress and you clear a Stress. __With Lock:__ Roll with advantage.
- **Plutonium Railgun** (metal): __Forward Facing.__ This card holds 3 __Reload Tokens__. __Spend 2 Reload Tokens__ to make a Weapons Action Roll against a target within Far range. On a success, deal Pd10+4 physical damage. __With Lock:__ Roll with advantage.
- **Gatling Autoturret** (metal): This card holds 4 __Reload Tokens__. When an adversary ship enters Very Close range of you, you can __spend a Reload Token__ to make a Weapons Reaction Roll against it. On a success, deal Pd4+6 physical damage.
- **Seeking Missile** (metal): This card holds 3 __Reload Tokens__. __With Lock: Spend 3 Reload Tokens__ to make the target temporarily *Sought*. At the start of each of its spotlights while it is *Sought*, make a Weapons Reaction Roll against it. On a success, deal Pd12 physical damage. *Sought* doesn't end when the __Lock__ does.
- **Ramming Cap** (metal): __Forward Facing.__ Move into Melee range of a target within Close range and make a Weapons Action Roll against it. On a success, deal Pd8 physical damage. You can then __mark a Hull Point__ to make the target mark a Hull Point. __While Available:__ You have an extra Weapons Hull Point.
- **Destructive Scan** (glass): Make a Weapons Action Roll against a target within Very Far range. On a success, deal Pd6 tech damage, and you can __mark a Stress__ to __Lock__ the target. __With Lock:__ On a success, it also becomes your __Studied Target__.
- **Dissection Ray** (glass): __Forward Facing.__ Make a Weapons Action Roll against your __Studied Target__. On a success, deal Pd4+6 tech damage. If it marks 2 or more Hull Points, it marks 1 more. __With Lock:__ Deal Pd8+6 instead.
- **Resonant Echo** (glass): Make a Weapons Action Roll against a target within Close range, with advantage if it is your __Studied Target__. On a success, you can __mark up to 3 Stress__. Deal 2d8 physical damage, plus 1d8 for each Stress marked. Then you can __spend Hope__ to clear Stress, one for one. __With Lock:__ The target marks Hull Points as if your ship were one size larger.
- **Critical Point Tracking** (glass): __With Lock:__ When you succeed on a Weapons Action Roll against your __Studied Target__, add 2d6 to the damage.
## Science

- **Manual Inspection** (flesh): __Spend a Hope__ to let a willing ship within Melee range clear a Hull Point.
- **Innervation** (flesh): Whenever you would gain Hope, you can clear that many Stress instead. __While Available:__ You have an extra Stress slot.
- **Slighted Spite** (flesh): When you or an ally within Close range takes damage from an adversary, you can __spend a Hope__ to __Lock__ that adversary.
- **Empathic Network** (flesh): __Mark a Stress__ to create a __Cloud__. When a ship in your __Cloud__ would mark Hull Points, you can __mark one of them instead__. If that damage was Severe, gain a Hope.
- **Anticipatory Neurons** (flesh): You have advantage on rolls to __Lock__. When a roll to __Lock__ gives you a Hope, one ally within Close range also gains a Hope. __While Available:__ You have an extra Stress slot.
- **Diagnosis Scanner** (mind): __Spend any number of Analysis Tokens__ to make a Science Action Roll (18), adding the number spent to the roll. On a success, an ally within Very Close range clears a Hull Point and clears a Stress for each token spent.
- **Optimized Routines** (mind): __Spend an Analysis Token__ and __spend a Hope__ to gain advantage on a roll. When you roll with Hope, you can gain an __Analysis Token__ instead of the Hope.
- **Warded Hull** (mind): When you would mark Hull Points from damage, you can first __spend 2 Analysis Tokens__ to reduce the severity by one threshold. __While Available:__ You have an extra Stress slot.
- **Opportunistic Analysis** (mind): When you deal damage to a ship, you can __spend an Analysis Token__ to make it __Vulnerable__ until the end of its next spotlight or yours, whichever comes first.
- **Detailed Systems Readout** (mind): When you __Lock__ a ship with an action roll, gain an __Analysis Token__. If you start a scene with no __Analysis Tokens__, gain one.
- **Synced Arcana** (mind): Add the trait you use for Science rolls to your damage thresholds, if it's positive.
- **Charge Extraction** (void): Once during your spotlight, when you make an adversary mark a Stress, you can __spend a Hope__ to clear a Stress.
- **Chaos Drive** (void): __Mark a Stress__ to add the GM's current Fear to a non-Weapons Action Roll.
- **Matter Inversion** (void): When you make an action roll with Fear, put an *Inversion Token* on this card (up to 3). __Spend an Inversion Token__ to choose the damage type of any damage dealt by or to a ship within Very Close range of you.
- **Electrified Field** (energy): __Mark a Stress__ to create a __Cloud__. Attacks made from inside your __Cloud__ deal an extra 1d8 damage on a success. The first time an attack from inside the __Cloud__ hits an adversary, gain a Hope.
- **Electron Beam** (energy): Make a Science Action Roll (13). On a success, you can __spend a Power Token__ to let a ship within Close range clear 2 Stress. Once per spotlight, you can __mark a Stress__ to gain a __Power Token__.
- **Oscillating Power Supply** (energy): Once per spotlight, you can __mark a Stress__ to gain 2 __Power Tokens__, or __spend 2 Power Tokens__ to clear a Stress. __While Available:__ You have an extra Stress slot.
- **Electrified Hull** (energy): __Spend a Power Token__ to make a willing ship within Melee range *Electrified*. The next time it docks with another ship, that ship marks a Hull Point and a Stress, and *Electrified* ends.
- **Close Range Scanning Battery** (metal): __Mark a Stress__ to make a Science Action Roll against a target within Close range. On a success, it is __Locked__ and takes Pd4 physical damage.
- **Hull Cannibalization** (metal): Once per __Refuel__, __mark a Hull Point__ to clear 3 Stress. __While Available:__ You have an extra Science Hull Point.
- **Pulse Beacon** (metal): __Mark a Hull Point__ to deploy a *Pulse Beacon*: a size 1 object with Evasion 15, your damage thresholds and 5 HP. You can move the Beacon to a point within Close range instead of moving your ship. When you deploy or move it, you can make a Science Action Roll against each ship within Close range of the Beacon. Ships you succeed against are __Vulnerable__ while within Close range of it, and you can __Lock__ one of them.
- **Advanced Targeting** (glass): When you __Lock__ a ship, you can __mark a Stress__ to make it your __Studied Target__. At the start of your spotlight, you can __Lock__ your __Studied Target__ as a reaction.
- **Sensor Mirage** (glass): When an ally within Close range becomes __Locked__, you can make a Science Action Roll against the highest Difficulty among adversaries. On a success, the __Lock__ ends, and the roll counts as a success with Hope. On a failure, it counts as a failure with Fear.
- **Phase Alignment** (glass): When your __Studied Target__ deals tech damage to a ship, that ship can __spend a Hope__ to reduce the severity by one threshold.
- **Gravitational Lens Drive** (glass): Allies' attacks can pass through your ship. While you have any unmarked Stress, physical and tech attacks you choose that pass within Very Close range of you add 1 to their Proficiency.
- **Quantum Entanglement** (glass): Your __Studied Target__ doesn't end until you choose, even when the scene ends. You always know the direction of your __Studied Target__ and how many unmarked Stress it has, at any distance.
- **Scanning Array** (glass): __Mark a Stress__ to create a __Cloud__. At the start of their spotlights, allies can __Lock__ any target in your __Cloud__ as a reaction. When the __Cloud__ dissipates, you can make any ship in it your __Studied Target__.
## Engineering

- **Neural Interface** (flesh): When you have no Hope and gain any Hope, clear a Stress. __While Available:__ You have an extra Stress slot.
- **Corrosive Slime** (flesh): Once per spotlight, when you __make contact__ with a ship, you can mark a Stress to lower its Major threshold by 2 and its Severe threshold by 4 for the rest of the scene.
- **Selective Shedding** (flesh): When you __make contact__ with a ship, you can __spend a Hope__ to slow it: until the end of its next spotlight, it can only move to a point within Very Close range. In a __Chase__, it moves back one band instead.
- **Arcanic Connection** (mind): Once per __Refuel__, the pilot can cast one of their spells through your ship. Make an Engineering Action Roll in place of the Spellcast Roll. The spell can affect ships your size or smaller as it would affect adversaries, at ship ranges.
- **Eldritch Breeze** (mind): Make an Engineering Action Roll against a target within Far range. On a success, it is temporarily caught in *Unfair Winds*. When it moves, you can __spend a Hope__ or an __Analysis Token__ to push it to a point within Very Close range of where it stopped. If you don't, *Unfair Winds* ends.
- **Rift Generator** (mind): __Mark a Stress__ to make an Engineering Action Roll (13). On a success, teleport to a point within Very Far range. For each ally within Very Close range you bring with you, __mark another Stress or 2 Hull Points__.
- **Pocket Dimension** (mind): __Mark a Stress__ to make an Engineering Action Roll (16). On a success, you open a pocket dimension holding an abandoned space station. It lasts long enough for one __Refuel__, and a __Refuel__ there clears half as much (round down). Characters can rest there undisturbed. Messages can pass in or out only if you allow it.
- **Boarding Glyph** (mind): __Mark a Stress__ to make an Engineering Action Roll against a ship within Very Close range (12 if it is willing). On a success, mark it with a *Boarding Glyph* and choose a place on your ship. Creatures you allow can travel between that place and a matching place on the marked ship (bridge to bridge, cargo bay to cargo bay). When a creature you forbid tries, make an Engineering Reaction Roll against it; on a success, it takes Pd8 magic damage and doesn't cross. The Glyph lasts while your ship has a pilot, your Engineering system isn't __Breached__, and the marked ship is within Far range.
- **Cloaking Field** (void): __Mark a Stress__ to become *Cloaked* until you make a Weapons or Transport Action Roll. While *Cloaked*, adversaries can't target you, and your first attack has advantage. Once per adversary spotlight, the GM can spend a Fear to reveal you; you can __spend a Hope__ to stay *Cloaked*.
- **Graviton Mine** (void): Make an Engineering Action Roll (15). On a success, you can __mark a Stress__ and __mark a Hull Point__ to place a *Graviton Mine* at a point within Close range. Other ships within Close range of the Mine are temporarily __Restrained__. Each of them that starts its spotlight within Close range of the Mine makes a Transport Reaction Roll (10 + the GM's current Fear): on a failure, it is pulled to the Mine; on a success, it is no longer __Restrained__. At the start of your next spotlight, every ship at the Mine takes Pd10+4 physical damage, ignoring size, and the Mine and its __Restrained__ condition end.
- **Void Attuned** (void): While you have 4 or more Void cards in your loadout, you have resistance to magic damage, and you clear a Stress whenever you take magic damage.
- **Scrap Hound** (void): Once per __Refuel__, when an adversary ship is destroyed, you can clear Hull Points equal to 1d4 + its size.
- **Veil of Night** (void): Make an Engineering Action Roll (13) to create a *Veil of Night*: a flat plane through the battlefield, reaching Very Far range in every direction. When a ship passes through it, you can __mark a Stress__ or __spend an Analysis Token__ to make that ship mark a Hull Point. Communications, light, sensing, tech damage and magic damage can't pass through the Veil. You can dismiss it at the end of any spotlight; it ends with the scene.
- **Siphon Array** (energy): When a ship within Very Close range marks a Stress, you can __spend a Hope__ to gain 2 __Power Tokens__. If you start a scene with no __Power Tokens__, gain 2.
- **Position Scrambler** (energy): __Mark a Stress__ to put 3 *Scrambler Tokens* on this card. When an adversary attacks you, you can __spend a Scrambler Token__ or a __Power Token__ to give the attack disadvantage.
- **Pulsar Mine** (energy): Make an Engineering Action Roll (15). On a success, you can __mark a Stress__ and __mark a Hull Point__ to place a *Pulsar Mine* at a point within Close range. Other ships within Close range of it take Pd6 tech damage. The Mine is removed at the start of your next spotlight. If you have a card that creates a __Cloud__, you can create that __Cloud__ at the Mine's location without paying its cost.
- **Energy Drain** (energy): Make an Engineering Action Roll against a ship within Very Close range. On a success, it marks a Stress and you clear a Stress.
- **Overload Barrage** (energy): __Mark a Stress__ to make an Engineering Action Roll against a ship within Close range. On a success, it is *Overloaded* until it next attacks or the scene ends. When an *Overloaded* ship attacks, the attack has disadvantage and the ship takes Pd6 tech damage.
- **Shrapnel Mine** (metal): Make an Engineering Action Roll (15). On a success, you can __mark a Stress__ and __mark a Hull Point__ to put a *Shrapnel Mine Token* on this card. When an adversary within Close range of you moves, you can __spend the token__ to deal Pd12+5 physical damage to it. At the start of any later spotlight, you can __discard the token__ to clear a Stress and a Hull Point.
- **Docking Winch** (metal): You have advantage on rolls to dock. You can give rolls to undock from your ship disadvantage.
- **Counterfire** (metal): This card holds 2 __Reload Tokens__. When an adversary attacks you, before damage is rolled, you can __spend any number of Reload Tokens__ as a reaction. Each token removes one damage die from the attack, to a minimum of 1 die.
- **Undercoat** (metal): At the start of your spotlight, add the number of your marked Hull Points to your damage thresholds until your next spotlight.
- **Lightweight Frame** (metal): You have +2 Evasion for each of your __Breached__ systems.
- **Orbiting Plate** (metal): __Mark a Hull Point__ to create an *Orbiting Plate*. When an attack roll beats your Evasion by less than 5, you can __destroy the Plate__ to take no damage from it. During your spotlight, you can __destroy the Plate__ to clear a Hull Point instead.
- **Projection Field** (glass): When you or allies you choose make an action roll against your __Studied Target__, treat it as one range closer.
- **Parallax Protocol** (glass): When an adversary attacks you, you can __mark a Stress__ to gain +3 Evasion against that attack. If the attacker is your __Studied Target__, you can then clear a Stress.
- **Ultraviolet Imaging** (glass): __Spend a Hope__ to make an Engineering Action Roll against a target within Very Far range. On a success, it is *Highlighted* until it leaves Very Far range or you use this card on another target. A *Highlighted* ship is __Vulnerable__ and can't become __Hidden__ or *Cloaked* unless something physically blocks it. If you rolled with Hope, you can also make it your __Studied Target__.
