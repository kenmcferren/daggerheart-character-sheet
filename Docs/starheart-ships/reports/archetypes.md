# Ship archetypes: power check (2026-11-08)

Hand estimate with the Series 14 model (`scripts/starheart-ship-power.mjs`): Tier 1 pilot, Proficiency 1, trait +2, target Difficulty 12, thresholds 6/12. Not a simulation; numbers are for ranking, not for balance to the decimal. Yardstick: Phoenix's rate, **3 Power Tokens save 1 HP = 0.33 HP per token**.

| Archetype | Rating | Why |
| :-- | :-: | :-- |
| Alchemist | Strong | +1 Proficiency on a Tier 1 attack (1d8+4 to 2d8+4) is about +0.67 HP per hit, +0.5 per attack, at 1 token. Each success gives a token back, so in steady play the +1 Proficiency is free. 1.5 to 2 times Phoenix per token, plus it refunds itself. |
| Champion | Strong | The ally redirect has no cost and no limit. One Champion can take every direct attack aimed at allies within Very Close. The Hope-for-HP trade is cheap but once per scene. |
| Raiju | Strong | 2 tokens buy a Far move plus an extra Stress on the target (about 0.5 HP per token plus the move), and every approach refunds a token. Needs a Studied Target. |
| Lich | Good, spiky | Free Stress on the target each Fear spotlight (needs Close range). The refuel heal is the GM's whole Fear pool: 0 to 12, usually 3 to 5, so it can be nearly a full heal once a day. |
| Phoenix | Baseline | Every HP marked gives a token; 3 tokens save 1 HP once per spotlight. Feeds other Power cards. |
| Drake | Baseline | One token per hit within Very Close; 2 tokens for 1 Hope, once per spotlight, so at most 1 Hope per spotlight. |
| Revenant | Baseline | Refill a card whenever you mark HP; Proficiency rises with each Breached system, which is the cost of getting hurt. Swingy but self-correcting. |
| Wraith | Baseline | 1 token = +1 on one roll (about +4 to 5% to hit), so no better than a Hope spend. The free token per Fear spend keeps it topped up. |
| Leviathan | Baseline, low | Always-on +1 (action rolls with Hope, reaction rolls without). Steady, small, no cost. |
| Necromancer | Situational | Rescue only matters when an ally is Disabled nearby. Spending the GM's Fear as your Hope is strong at the table but costs the GM, not you. |
| Chirurgeon | Weak to baseline | Marking a Hull Point to turn a miss into a hit costs about 1 HP for about 1.3 HP of damage and gives the GM a Fear. The once-per-Refuel heal is good but needs the target within Very Close. |
| Direwolf | Spiky | 3 Hope is half the Hope track, but only when the Studied Target goes down: about once a fight. One feature only, by design. |
| Specter | Niche | 3 Power Tokens for a full round of immunity, but it can't deal damage that round. Useful for a Disabled-edge escape. |
| Elemental | Weak | Resistance to tech damage only. Worth a token only against tech attackers. Converting tokens is flexible but gains nothing. |
| Golem | Weak | Each token cuts damage by the pilot's tier: about 1 in 6 chance to drop a threshold step, roughly 0.17 HP per token at every tier (the band widens with tier as fast as the cut). Half of Phoenix, and a token is gained only when an adversary attack marks HP. |

## Rules gaps to settle

- **Necromancer:** whose HP does "clear a HP" clear? Reading used for balance: the Disabled ship's. Does "a ship in Very Close range" include adversaries?
- **Champion:** no cost, no limit, no roll. Consider once per spotlight, or a Stress mark.
- **Alchemist:** "each can be chosen only once" is printed as "choose a different benefit for each token". With a token back per success the cap of 3 rarely binds.
- **Elemental:** resistance to tech damage only; adversary ships may rarely deal it.
- **Chirurgeon:** "succeed with Fear" gives the GM a Fear; check that is intended.
- **Revenant:** "reload one domain card" is printed as refilling that card's Reload Tokens, with no cost.

## Owner rulings and changes applied (2026-11-08)

- **Necromancer:** the HP clear works on the Disabled ship or on the Necromancer's own ship, whichever they choose; any ship within Very Close counts.
- **Champion:** stays unlimited.
- **Alchemist:** the token comes only on a success with Hope (a critical success counts).
- **Golem:** unchanged.
- **Elemental:** each Refuel, choose a damage type; a Power Token gives resistance to that type against one instance of damage.
- **Chirurgeon:** unchanged (Hull Point cost, for the Flesh theme).
