# Starfinder Communities: Pact Worlds Draft

Format: `Planet - Background`, then two sentences (the place, then how its people carry it), matching the owner's Hubborne example. No mechanics. Facts from the Starfinder Wiki (Pact Worlds and per-world pages), reworded in original phrasing (reworded again 2026-10-24 to stay clear of the source text); not checked against Starfinder 2e. Draft for owner review.

1. **Absalom Station - Hubborne**
   Floating in Lost Golarion's empty orbit, this bustling space station is the center of interstellar trade and governance among the Pact Worlds. The people who live here are accustomed to the hustle and bustle and are always looking for their next opportunity.
  **Well Connected**: At the start of a rest, refuel, or refit, gain tokens equal to the number of players at the table. At the end of the rest, refuel, or refit, distribute tokens as you'd like to yourself and/or any other players. Each token clears an armor, stress, or HP, or grants a Hope.

2. **Absalom Station - Spikeborn**
   The Spike is the cramped, lawless underside of the station, beneath the wealthy Eye and next to the reactor. Its children learn the hidden passages, whose favor is for sale, and whose debts to avoid.
   **One Eye Open**: Once per scene, while you are hidden, you may mark a Stress to gain a Hope.

3. **Akiton - Scrapborn**
   Akiton's dry wastes are littered with wrecked ships, played-out mines, and feuding cities. Its people live off salvage and oddities pulled from the wreckage, and believe only what they have tried themselves.
   **Upcycling**: Each new scene containing scraps, garbage, or fallen adversaries, you may collect a scrap token. Spend tokens during a rest as follows: Spend tokens as a bonus to your scrap roll, and spend tokens to roll that many dice during your scrap roll. Roll scrap dice, add the bonus to each, and pick your favorite result from the consumable table.

4. **Akiton - Townbound**
   Many on Akiton live in settlements owned by a single company that supplies the home, the shop, and the paycheck. They know long shifts, mounting debt, and the quiet ways workers cover for each other.
   **I've Got Your Back**: Once per rest you can encourage an ally with kind words or action. You gain a Hope and they clear up to 3 Stress.

5. **Castrovel - Asanan**
   In the lashunta cities of Asana, universities and businesses share the same busy streets, and a major spaceport links them to the system. Locals treat learning as a form of wealth and skill as a vocation.
   **Asanan Learner**: Gain the experience "Asanan Learner". It always has a bonus equal to your highest experience bonus, and applies to any non-violent trait. When used, reduce it by 1. At each long rest, restore it to full.

6. **Castrovel - Wardenborn**
   Castrovel's wild preserves are lovely and dangerous, and the Xenowardens guard them. Those raised in their care learn to defend wildlife and to give it its due respect.
   **Wildsense**: You know the ways of non-sapient lifeforms. When attacked by them, gain +2 evasion. When attacking them, you can force them to mark Stress instead of HP, one for one. With a Presence Action roll success you can force all that are Vulnerable within Close range of you to flee.

7. **Castrovel - Hivebound**
   The formian Colonies are cooperating hives devoted to the Overqueen and built around making things. Their people prize teamwork, defined duties, and tasks done right.
   **Duty Bound**: Each rest, discuss what your new duty should be, in a one sentence description. When acting under this duty you can spend a hope for +2 to an action roll. On a success, gain a Hope.

8. **Verces - Ringborn**
   Verces has a blazing side and a frozen side, and its population crowds into the thin habitable band between, shared by 27 nations. Natives are skilled with machines and implants, and used to debating over borders.
   **Negotiator**: You have advantage to make a compromise when you are willing to give something up. If no compromise is reached you can spend a Hope to force the GM to lose a Fear.

9. **Verces - Fringeborn**
   Beyond the Ring lie the Outlaw Kingdoms, a refuge for exiles, fugitives, cultists, and survivors of the desert and the ice. Those raised there follow their own codes and expect nothing from the Ring's authorities.
   **Rulebreaker**: With a moment of observation you gain a sense of who is in charge and what they generally want. You can sense tension in the ranks if it exists. Spend a Hope and a moment of conversation to gain a small favor if you act on this knowledge.

10. **Triaxus - Legionborn**
    Triaxus endures long, harsh winters, and the Skyfire Legion is the mercenary host that guards its lands and sells its swords across the system. Those who grew up in its shadow can hold a line and read a contract.
	**Front Line**: You may make a Reaction Roll appropriate to the situation to stop an adversary's movement when they are within Very Close range, or to force it to target you instead of something behind you.

11. **Bretheda - Cloudborn**
    Bretheda's people dwell in floating cities high in a gas giant's air, under the barathu Confluence. They watch the winds, scan the sky, and keep steady when the weather turns.
	**Weather Vein**: Once per spotlight spend a Hope to lose a mental condition, or to clear a mental condition on an Ally in Very Close range.

12. **Bretheda - Moonbound**
    The moons around Bretheda include industrial water worlds, crystal caverns, and gem mines worked by bonded and convicted labor. Raised there, you learn risky work, tough deals, and who is quietly buying their way out.
	**Worker's Bond**: You have advantage when speaking to the working or lower class about what's *really* going on, and when examining a work site for abnormalities, exits, and opportunities.	

13. **Liavara - Roselit**
    Roselight is a floating platform-city that mines gas from Liavara, where quotas and barathu firms set the pace. Its people tend floating gardens and know how to keep a sealed living system going.
	**Self Contained**: At the start of each session gain Rose tokens equal to your level. Spend a Rose token (A) once per spotlight to clear a Stress, (B) twice per rest to clear an Armor or HP for each, and (C) once per session to give yourself and one ally in Close range 1 Hope each.

14. **Apostae - Domeborn**
    Apostae has almost no air, so elven noble houses rule from enchanted domes while competing weapons makers drive the economy. Natives understand arms, corporate rivalry, and when to leave a question unasked.
	**Weapon Specialist**: You can switch weapons without marking a Stress. When using a tech weapon you can mark a Stress to reroll all damage dice.

15. **The Diaspora - Beltborn**
    The Diaspora is a scatter of asteroids and wreckage inhabited by dwarf miners, android refugees, monks, and Free Captain pirates. Its people are self-reliant, inventive, and used to scraping by with little law.
	**Wary**: When the first attack of a scene happens you can make an attack as a Reaction Roll. On a success, gain a Hope. You have advantage when trying to spot lies and hidden things.
