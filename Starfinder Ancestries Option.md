# Starfinder Ancestries

## Global rules
- Toggle label: "Use Starfinder ancestries"
- Mixed ancestry: disabled when toggle is on
- Show these ancestries first, in alphabetical order, then the other ancestries afterwards.
- Remove Clank and Human from the list if the startfinder ancestries are checked.

## Android
- With Purpose: New experiences start at +3 instead of +2.
- Constructed: You have advantage to resist natural effects like heat, cold, disease, poison, and radiation.


## Baruthu
- Reconfigure: When you would take Major or greater damage, you may Mark a Stres to reduce the damage threshold by 1.
- Floating: You can fly. Mark a Stress to propel yourself a far distance without needing the standard Agility Trait roll.

## Human
(as per Human rules in Daggerheart)

## Kasatha 
- Reliable: when you assist an ally by spending a Hope you gain a Hope if they succeed.
- Four-armed: You can wield an additional secondary weapon. Gain the benefits of all secondary weapons. Passive and reactive benefits stack where reasonable.

## Lashunta
- Limited Telepathy: Spend a Hope to allow willing targets in far range of you to communicate with you and each other through your mind. You hear all of this conversation. This effect ends for each target as they leave far range or take a long rest.
- Open Minded: Once per rest ( ) you can extend your telepathic abilities to an unwilling target in Far range. Mark a Stress to roll a number of D6s equal to your Presence or Instinct trait. If you roll any 4s, learn what they fear now. Any 5s: what they'll do next. Any 6s: what they wish to keep secret.

## Pahtra
- Nimble: You have advantage on Agility, Finesse, and Insight reaction rolls.
- Retractable Claws: Your claws are an Agility or Finesse Melee weapon dealing d6 phyiscal damage using your proficiency. Once per rest, when an attack using your claws succeeds with Hope gain an additional Hope.

## Shirren
- Universal Telepathy: Spend a Hope to communicate with a target in Close range. They can understand you, though they are not compelled to respond. You can understand them regardless of language or sapience barrier.
- Encouraging Vibes: When a target in Close range fails an action roll you can mark a Stres to allow them to reroll either die.

## Skittermander
- Hug Master: As an action you can make an Agility roll against a target larger than yourself to climb it. On a success, they temporarily gain the Hugged condition. While Hugged: they cannot attack you, but can force an Agility Reaction roll from you to free themsevles; you move with them; attacks against you that miss hit them; mark a Stress as a reaction to impose disadvantage on actions they take. Hugged ends when you leave melee range, you fail the reaction roll above, or the GM spends a fear to end it.
- Tiny Chaotic Target: Gain a +1 to your Evasion.

## Vesk 
- Blood Sense: Spend a Hope to hone in on biological scents nearby. Biological or partially biological creatures in far range from you cannot hide from you and you have advantage on action rolls to follow their trail.
- Cradled by War: When your attack against an adversary succeeds, mark a Stress to gain a Hope. When an attack against you fails, spend a Hope to force your attacker to mark a Hit Point.

## Ysoki
- Scurry: When you move, gain the Scurrying condition. Lose Scurrying at the start of your next Spotlight. While Scurrying, you have +2 evasion.
- Bite-Sized: You have sharp teeth that are a Strength or Finesse Melee weapon dealing d4+2 using your proficiency. You can mark a Stress to chew through thin metal, plastic, fibers, etc. Depending on thickness this may require an action roll. If you successfully damage an adversary with a bite, mark a Stress to give them the Chewed condition, which lowers their thresholds by 3 each.
