# Armor — Tier 4 (Levels 8–10)

> Source: Daggerheart SRD 2.0, Armor Tables, pp. 74–74

Base Thresholds are Major / Severe damage thresholds (add your level to both). Rules: [Armor.md](Armor.md). You can't equip armor of a higher tier than you.
