# Armor — Tier 1 (Level 1)

> Source: Daggerheart SRD 2.0, Armor Tables, pp. 72–72

Base Thresholds are Major / Severe damage thresholds (add your level to both). Rules: [Armor.md](Armor.md). You can't equip armor of a higher tier than you.

| Name | Base Thresholds (Major / Severe) | Base Armor Score | Feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mage Robes | 4 / 10 | 2 | Enchanted: Gain a bonus to your damage thresholds equal to your Spellcast trait. |
| Gambeson Armor | 5 / 11 | 3 | Flexible: +1 to Evasion |
| Brigandine Armor | 6 / 12 | 3 | Lined: Mark a Stress to negate Minor damage. |
| Leather Armor | 6 / 13 | 3 | — |
| Scale Mail Armor | 7 / 14 | 3 | Cumbersome: −1 to Finesse |
| Chainmail Armor | 7 / 15 | 4 | Heavy: −1 to Evasion |
| Banded Armor | 8 / 16 | 4 | Bulky: −1 to Evasion; when you take Severe damage, you must mark a Stress. |
| Full Plate Armor | 8 / 17 | 4 | Very Heavy: −2 to Evasion; −1 to Agility |
