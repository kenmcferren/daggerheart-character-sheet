> Source: Daggerheart SRD 2.0, Secondary Weapon Tables, pp. 68–68

Weapon stat definitions: [Equipment.md](Equipment.md). Damage is dice plus a flat modifier; you roll a number of dice equal to your Proficiency. `phy` = physical damage, `mag` = magic damage.
