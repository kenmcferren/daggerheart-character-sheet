# Armor — Tier 2 (Levels 2–4)

> Source: Daggerheart SRD 2.0, Armor Tables, pp. 72–72

Base Thresholds are Major / Severe damage thresholds (add your level to both). Rules: [Armor.md](Armor.md). You can't equip armor of a higher tier than you.

| Name | Base Thresholds (Major / Severe) | Base Armor Score | Feature |
|---|---|---|---|

| Improved Gambeson Armor | 7 / 16 | 4 | Flexible: +1 to Evasion |
| Improved Brigandine Armor | 9 / 19 | 4 | Lined: Mark a Stress to negate Minor damage. |
| Improved Leather Armor | 9 / 20 | 4 | — |
| Improved Scale Mail Armor | 11 / 23 | 4 | Cumbersome: −1 to Finesse |
| Improved Chainmail Armor | 11 / 24 | 5 | Heavy: −1 to Evasion |

| Improved Full Plate Armor | 13 / 28 | 5 | Very Heavy: −2 to Evasion; −1 to Agility |
| Enchanter’s Robes | 9 / 20 | 4 | Mnemonic: Once per scene, you can recall a domain card from your vault without paying its Recall Cost. |
| Hawkguard’s Mantle | 9 / 20 | 4 | Gliding: You can glide up to Far range and are immune to damage from falling. |
| Spidersilk Tunic | 9 / 20 | 4 | Wall-Crawling: +1 Evasion; you can walk on walls as easily as on the ground. |
