# Domains

> Source: Daggerheart SRD 2.0, p. 7

There are ten domains, each a collection of cards granting features or special abilities around a theme. (The Daggerheart Core Set includes only Arcana, Blade, Bone, Codex, Grace, Midnight, Sage, Splendor, and Valor; this SRD also includes Dread.)

PCs acquire two 1st-level domain cards at character creation and one more domain card at or below their level each time they level up. Each class grants access to two domains.
