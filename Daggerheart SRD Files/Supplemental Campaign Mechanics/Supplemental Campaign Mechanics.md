# Supplemental Campaign Mechanics

> Source: Daggerheart SRD 2.0, p. 190

You can use following optional mechanics to supplement your campaigns.

## Files

- [Faction Tracking](Faction Tracking.md) — Faction cards, relationships, objective countdowns.
- [Everyday Hero Starting Equipment](Everyday Hero Starting Equipment.md) — Mundane starting equipment for everyday-hero campaigns.
- [Feasts](Feasts.md) — Ingredients, cooking meals, restaurants.
- [Grimdark Campaigns](Grimdark Campaigns.md) — Shadow-touched adversaries, sacred bonfires and torches.
- [Tech-Based Campaigns](Tech-Based Campaigns.md) — Tech damage, iconic weapons, crafting and trading scrap.
- [Western Campaigns](Western Campaigns.md) — Weapons and loot for western campaigns.
- [Colossal Adversaries](Colossal Adversaries.md) — Segmented colossus adversaries and how to run them.
- [Floating Magic School Campaigns](Floating Magic School Campaigns.md) — Flight and less lethal campaigns.
- [Fairy Tale Campaigns](Fairy Tale Campaigns.md) — Curses, transforming adversaries, building villains collaboratively.
- [Monster Hunting Campaigns](Monster Hunting Campaigns.md) — Equipment, transformations, the hunt, making a monster.
- [Hex Crawl Campaigns](Hex Crawl Campaigns.md) — Hex maps, travel, resources, encounters, doom tracks.
